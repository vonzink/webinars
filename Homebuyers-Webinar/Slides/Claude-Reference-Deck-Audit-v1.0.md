## What Works

1. Strong opening tone
   - Dark green cover feels premium and confident.
   - The title is large, simple, and easy to read.
   - The promise is concrete: a short session can save buyers thousands.

2. Clear rhythm between dark and light slides
   - Dark slides work well for section framing and emotional emphasis.
   - White slides work well for analysis, diagrams, and instruction.
   - The deck avoids feeling like a worksheet because it changes visual mode at the right moments.

3. Good information restraint
   - Most slides have one job.
   - The best slides use one dominant visual: roadmap, myth grid, payment donut, timeline, split rent/own comparison.
   - The copy is short enough to support the presenter instead of replacing them.

4. Effective MSFG brand expression
   - The palette is disciplined: deep green, bright green, soft neutral backgrounds.
   - The bright green is used as a signal, not as a blanket treatment.
   - The logo is mostly used on white or controlled areas, which avoids cheapening the brand.

5. Useful seminar architecture
   - Roadmap slide creates orientation.
   - Myth slide immediately answers objections.
   - Payment slide teaches visually.
   - Mistakes slide creates practical value.
   - CTA slide feels direct but not aggressive.

## What To Carry Into The 15-Slide Version

- Use a dark green title slide with one bright green word or line.
- Keep the 15-slide structure visually calm: dark cover, white teaching slides, dark section/emotional beats, white CTA.
- Include one strong myth slide early in the deck.
- Use the payment donut/stack concept for the payment explanation.
- Use the clean timeline style for the process or seven-day plan.
- Use the split white/deep-green layout for the final CTA.
- Keep the large, left-aligned title treatment.
- Keep footer/licensing small and quiet.

## What To Improve For The MSFG Presentation System

- Make calculator placeholders more integrated and premium.
- Replace generic cards with purpose-built graphics where possible.
- Reduce text on dense card-grid slides.
- Build stronger transitions between topic sections.
- Add explicit live audience interaction moments.
- Add speaker-note guidance for each slide.
- Preserve the dark/white rhythm without copying the PPTX as a template.

## Recommended 15-Slide Style Direction

The focused deck should feel like this source deck upgraded into a more deliberate keynote:

- Same confidence.
- More narrative.
- More live calculation moments.
- Fewer generic cards.
- More diagrams that show the idea immediately.
- Stronger educator posture.

The deck should not become busier than the source. Its best quality is restraint.
