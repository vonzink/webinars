## Seminar Thesis

First-time buyers do not need a sales pitch. They need a clear way to answer:

**Can I actually buy a home, and what should I do next?**

The seminar teaches one complete homebuyer journey through one consistent example, live calculator moments, visual explanations, and calm decision points.

## Design Posture

- Educator, not salesperson
- One idea per slide
- Show the math visually
- Use live calculators as teaching moments
- Avoid urgency, scarcity, and pressure
- Invite the next step without forcing it
