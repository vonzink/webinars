# Presenter Runbook v1.0

# The Homebuyer's Playbook

## Presenter Role

The presenter is an educator and guide. The presenter should look and sound like the person who makes a complicated decision easier, not the person trying to close a lead.

## Core Language

Use:

- "Let's make the number visible."
- "This may or may not be right for you."
- "A good lender should be willing to explain this."
- "The purpose is clarity, not pressure."
- "This is hypothetical until we run your actual numbers."

Avoid:

- "Rates are going up."
- "You need to act now."
- "Everyone should buy."
- "This is easy."
- "Guaranteed."

## Interaction Rhythm

Ask for participation every 4 to 5 slides:

- Slide 001: emotional temperature
- Slide 002: lender experience poll
- Slide 006: current rent prompt
- Slide 011: calculator input choice
- Slide 016: approved vs comfortable reflection
- Slide 019: down payment myth poll
- Slide 025: worksheet exercise
- Slide 031: APR comparison vote
- Slide 036: credit myth prompt
- Slide 043: confidence check

## Calculator Delivery Pattern

1. Name the question.
2. Show the inputs.
3. Run one example.
4. Change one variable.
5. State the takeaway.
6. Return to the story.

## Compliance Reminders

- All numbers are hypothetical unless reviewed for the event.
- Do not quote live rates unless the presentation has been specifically approved for that use.
- Do not promise approval, savings, program eligibility, or assistance.
- Use the NMLS and Equal Housing footer where required.
- Verify program and assistance details on the delivery date.

## Ending Posture

The close should feel like an invitation:

"Bring us your questions before you bring us your application."

The audience should leave with confidence, not urgency.
