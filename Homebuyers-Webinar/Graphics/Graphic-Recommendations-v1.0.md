## Visual Rules

- Use official MSFG colors only.
- Use one icon family across the entire seminar.
- Prefer diagrams, cards, timelines, and control panels over paragraphs.
- Keep every graphic editable.
- Avoid screenshots of legal forms unless compliance approves the exact use.
- Use illustrations as explanations, not decorations.
