Editing a slide

      
    
        
  1. Pick a slide in the list on the left. It comes up in the middle.
    2. 
        
  2. Click any text on the slide and type, or change the HTML, Slide CSS and
          Slide JS boxes on the right. The slide updates as you type.
    3. 
        
  3. Press Save for everyone. Until then nothing changes for anyone else.
    4. 
      

      
Add a slide puts a copy of the current slide right after it, ready to change.
        Delete this slide takes it out of the deck; deleted deck slides wait at the bottom of the list
        so you can bring them back. To change the order, drag a slide up or down the list, or use
        Move up and Move down. Reset to original undoes a saved edit.


      
Building a slide with Claude or ChatGPT

      
    
        
  1. Open the slide you want to change (or add a new one), then press Copy the prompt below.
    2. 
        
  2. Paste it into Claude or ChatGPT and replace the last line with what you want the slide to be.
    3. 
        
  3. It answers with three blocks. Paste them into the HTML, Slide CSS and
          Slide JS boxes, check the slide in the middle, then save.
    4. 
      

      

        
        
      

      

      
Building several slides at once

      
    
        
  1. Press Copy the multi-slide prompt, paste it into Claude or ChatGPT, and replace the last
          line with what the presentation should cover.
    2. 
        
  2. It answers with one block of code. Copy that block and paste it into the box below.
    3. 
        
  3. Press Add these slides. They go in after the slide you have open, and are saved for
          everyone straight away. Delete any slides you no longer want afterwards.
    4.
