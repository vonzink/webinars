# Company Philosophy

Education before sales.

Trust before transactions.

Relationships over volume.

Our objective is to become our client's mortgage advisor for life.

We believe informed borrowers make better financial decisions.

Our presentations should never feel like sales seminars.

They should feel like attending a premium educational workshop taught by financial professionals.

---

# Presentation Style

Everything should communicate:

Professionalism

Trust

Transparency

Expertise

Modern Technology

Premium Financial Services

Our presentations should resemble Apple Keynotes, TED Talks, and modern financial conferences—not generic PowerPoint templates.

Design should be:

• Modern
• Elegant
• Editorial
• Minimal
• Sophisticated
• High-end
• Confident
• Educational

Avoid:

❌ Clip art

❌ Cheap stock photos

❌ WordArt

❌ Busy layouts

❌ Generic icons

❌ Decorative shapes without purpose

White space is encouraged.

Typography should be the primary design element.

# Compliance

Always display NMLS information when presenter information is shown.

Use Equal Housing Opportunity branding where appropriate.

Never omit required licensing information.

Avoid making promises or guarantees regarding loan approval or interest rates.

All mortgage information should be educational in nature.

---

# Final Objective

Every slide should answer one question:

"If I were attending a $2,000 executive seminar on home buying, would this slide feel worthy of that experience?"

If the answer is no—

Redesign it.
