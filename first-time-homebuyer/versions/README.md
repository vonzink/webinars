## Six decisions that are not up for revision

Recorded in `v4/CHANGELOG.md`, repeated here because they get re-litigated:

1. **One canonical example throughout.** Changing it breaks 30+ slides.
2. **Calculators teach; they never close.**
3. **The unconditional CTA stays** — free Loan Estimate review, any lender, no
   obligation. Cut it and the webinar becomes the thing it was built to replace.
4. **No urgency language.** Slide 005 promises this on camera.
5. **Every module ends in a portable artifact.**
6. **Objections that cut against the presenter's interest stay in.**

## When to open a v5

Open one when a *structural* argument changes — not for a number update or a slide
tweak. Those are edits to v4.

Signals that warrant v5:
- Poll 5's confidence delta shrinking across deliveries
- The Loan-Estimate-review-to-booked-call ratio inverting
  (`PROJECT.md` § success criterion 5 — this means it drifted into a funnel)
- A rule change that invalidates a module's argument
- Consistent overrun that compression can't fix

**Write the critique first, then the version.** That order is the method.
