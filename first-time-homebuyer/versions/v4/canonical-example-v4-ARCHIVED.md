# THE CANONICAL EXAMPLE — single source of truth

> **Rule: this webinar uses exactly one house and one loan, from minute 3 to
> minute 40.** Every module adds a layer to the same numbers. Do not introduce a
> second property. If you change a number here, you must update every slide that
> references it.

All figures are **hypothetical and for education only**. Not a quote, not an offer,
not a commitment to lend. Actual terms depend on credit, property, program, market
conditions, and full underwriting.

## The buyers

**Maya and Dev** — composite characters, not real clients.
Combined gross income **$9,500/month** ($114,000/yr). Renting at $2,150/month.
Existing debts: car $415/mo, student loans $290/mo, credit card minimums $75/mo.
Total non-housing debt = **$780/month**. Mid FICO scores 742 and 758 → **742 used**.

**Teaching point:** closing costs were **$5,400**. Cash to close was **$31,760**.
Those are not the same word. This is the #1 cash-shock in the whole transaction.

Daily interest: $427,500 × 6.500% ÷ 365 = **$76.14/day**.

## The two-loan APR comparison (slides 44–49)

Same $427,500 loan, same house, two lenders.

| | Loan A | Loan B |
|---|---|---|
| Note rate | 6.750% | 6.250% |
| Discount points | 0 | 2.00 pts = **$8,550** |
| Other lender fees | $1,400 | $1,400 |
| **P&I** | **$2,773** | **$2,632** |
| Monthly difference | — | **−$141** |

**Breakeven = $8,550 ÷ $141 = 60.6 months ≈ 5 years 1 month.**

> Portable rule: *Points are a bet on how long you'll keep this exact loan. If you
> won't hold it past breakeven, you lit the money on fire.*

## The amortization demo (slide 51)

$427,500 @ 6.500%, 30-year fixed.

- First payment: **$2,315 interest / $387 principal** (83% of it is rent on money)
- Total interest over 30 years: **≈ $545,200**
- Crossover — the month principal finally exceeds interest: **month 218 (year 18)**
- **The $100 experiment:** add $100/month from day one →
  paid off in **~27.0 years instead of 30** and total interest **≈ $480,900**.
  **Saves ≈ $64,300 and ~3 years.**

## Mortgage insurance removal on this loan

- 80% LTV threshold = **$360,000** → requires **$67,500** of paydown or a
  qualifying appreciation-based reappraisal (lender/investor rules vary)
- 78% automatic termination = **$351,000**, by original amortization schedule
  (Homeowners Protection Act, primary residence, payments current)
