### 041 · Data + poll
**HEADLINE:** Utilization is 30% of your score. It moves in 30 days.
**ON SLIDE:**
Payment history ~35% *(slow)* · **Amounts owed ~30% (fast)** · History ~15% ·
New credit ~10% · Mix ~10%
**Poll: True or false — closing a card you don't use helps your score.**
**FALSE.** It shortens history and raises utilization.
Pay under 30% **per card**, ideally 10%. Don't close anything. Fix errors **early.**
**You don't need a 780.** Pricing moves in tiers.
**VISUAL:** Score donut by weight, utilization slice pulsing in Lime. Poll
overlays mid-slide.

# MODULE 6 — THE BUILDER (32:10–34:30)

### 043 · Data
**HEADLINE:** Don't negotiate price. Negotiate everything else.
**ON SLIDE:**
Builders would rather give **$20,000 in incentives** than take $20,000 off the price —
a price cut sets a comp.
Negotiable: closing costs · rate buydowns · upgrades · appliances · landscaping ·
fencing · lot premium
⚠️ **Your first-year taxes may be assessed on an unimproved lot.** Then it jumps —
and you get a bill for the shortfall.
**Ask in writing what the tax basis will be at full assessment.**
**VISUAL:** Two-column new-vs-existing comparison where the winning side highlights
per row — **and it genuinely alternates.** If one column wins every row, the graphic
is lying. Tax row gets a warning flag.
