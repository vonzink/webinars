### 021 · Data
**HEADLINE:** Four ways in.
**ON SLIDE:**
**Conventional 3%** — *first-time buyer = no primary residence in **3 years.** You can
have owned before.* Ask for **HomeReady** or **Home Possible** by name — reduced MI,
better pricing.
**FHA 3.5%** — forgiving on credit. Tradeoff: under 10% down, **MI lasts the life of
the loan.** Conventional's comes off.
**USDA 0%** — eligible areas + income limits. "Rural" doesn't mean a farm. Check the
address.
**VA 0%** — no monthly MI. Qualifies on **residual income.** Reusable. Guard and
Reserves count. *He was 34. Nobody had ever asked him.*
*Program guidelines. Lender overlays apply. Not an eligibility determination.*
**VISUAL:** Four program cards, identical template: *minimum · who it's for · the
tradeoff.* VA card in Lime. On the VA card, strike through the two biggest cost
lines — down payment and monthly MI — in Green.

### 022 · Build

③ **County** · varies widely
④ **Employer / profession** · teachers, first responders, healthcare, unions
