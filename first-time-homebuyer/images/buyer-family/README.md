The audience, as they actually are.

| Slide | Shot | Notes |

## Casting

The population is **23–45 and broader than the stereotype**: single buyers, couples,
co-buyers, people with kids, people without, and a genuine range of ages and
backgrounds. See the fair lending note in `../README.md`.

## What kills these shots

- Anyone smiling at the camera
- Visible brand-new furniture (this is the *before* photo, not the after)
- Perfect styling — a slightly messy table is the point
- Anything that looks like a bank ad
