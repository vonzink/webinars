## Notes

These images support the least glamorous content in the deck — the costs and risks
nobody puts in a calculator. **Shoot them straight.** No drama, no dread. The
information is sobering enough without help from the photography.

The roof shot in particular supports a specific line: *"nothing for two years, and
then a fourteen-thousand-dollar roof."*

## Compliance

- Nothing implying a specific defect rate or outcome
