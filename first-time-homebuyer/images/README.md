# images/

Shot lists per theme. One `README.md` per subfolder with the specific shots each
slide needs.

---

## The photography rule

**Real over stock.** The webinar's entire premise is that everyone else is selling
and this isn't. Stock photography of a beaming couple holding an oversized novelty
key undermines that in half a second, before a word is spoken.

**Use:** unposed moments · people looking at documents, laptops, spreadsheets ·
empty rooms · actual Colorado (foothills, Front Range neighborhoods, real streets) ·
natural and imperfect light · a genuine range of ages, household types, and
backgrounds

**Never use:** SOLD signs with a thumbs-up · giant novelty keys · people leaping ·
handshakes in front of houses · confetti · anything visibly watermarked · anything
implying one household type is the default buyer

---

## Fair lending note

The people shown across this deck must reflect the actual first-time buyer
population — a range of ages, household compositions, and backgrounds. This is both
an accuracy matter and a fair lending consideration. Reviewed in
`../prompts/05-compliance-review.md` § 5.

---

## Treatment

- Full-bleed where photography is the slide (stories, section titles)
- **Navy scrim at 60%+** wherever text sits over an image — verify contrast, don't
  eyeball it
- Desaturate slightly toward the deck palette; avoid heavily filtered looks
- Minimum 2560px wide for full-bleed at 1080p with room to reframe
