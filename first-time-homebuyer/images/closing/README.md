## The rule for the final image

Slide 070 closes a webinar whose entire argument is *no hype*. A triumphant
key-handoff photo would undo forty minutes of restraint in one frame.

**Keys on a counter. Empty room. Morning light. Nobody in the shot.**

## Compliance

- No real financial documents or amounts
- No identifiable title company or lender
