## Tone

The easy version of this module is "builders are trying to trap you." That's wrong,
self-serving, and the images shouldn't imply it either.

**New construction should look genuinely appealing in these shots.** The module's
credibility depends on the comparison being fair — including the story where the
builder's lender wins.

## Compliance

- 🔴 **No identifiable builder** — no signage, logos, or recognizable branding
- Nothing implying builder practices are improper
- Affiliated business arrangements are lawful and disclosed
