# Logo Usage — Mountain State Financial Group, LLC

Official mark received 2026-07-30. **Supersedes `logo.svg`, which was my placeholder
and is now void.**

## What the mark is

A triangle rendered as **five graduated bands**, running from **lime at the apex**
through mid-greens to **deep teal at the base**. Wordmark below: `MOUNTAIN` in lime +
`STATE` in deep teal, then `FINANCIAL` in lime + `GROUP, LLC` in deep teal. Carries ™.

## It confirms the palette

| Palette role | Where it appears in the mark |
|---|---|
| **Lime `#8cc63E`** | Triangle apex · "MOUNTAIN" · "FINANCIAL" |
| **Deep Teal `#104547`** | Triangle base · "STATE" · "GROUP, LLC" |
| **Green `#4b7b4d`** | The intermediate bands |

No palette change needed. The mark validates every assignment already in the handoff.

## ⭐ The band gradient is the deck's visual language

The apex-to-base gradient — **small/light at the top, large/dark at the base** — is
already how the deck's core graphics are structured. Use it deliberately:

- **The payment stack (009/010).** P&I is the largest segment in Deep Teal (the
  mountain's base); MI is the smallest in Lime (the apex). This mapping was chosen
  before the logo arrived and it lines up exactly. Render the stack's segment edges
  with the same hard-edged banding as the mark — no soft gradients.
- **The Gap graphic (003 → 047).** The mark's diagonal edge is a usable motif for the
  gap itself.
- **The amortization curve (038).** Banded fills rather than smooth gradients.

This is what makes the deck read as *theirs* rather than as a template with a logo
dropped in the corner.

## 🔴 The logo is exempt from the contrast rule. Nothing else is.

The wordmark sets **lime type on white at 2.1:1**. That is fine — logotypes are
explicitly exempt from WCAG 1.4.3.

**Do not "fix" the logo.** And do not read it as permission: lime remains a fill and
shape colour everywhere else in the deck. Body copy, headlines, and figures never use
it on a light ground.

## Placement

- **Footer** — horizontal lockup, small, Mid Gray-weight presence. Never larger than
  the presenter's name.
- **Section title slides** — Deep Teal field, so a **white knockout** version is required.
- **Slides 001 and 047 — NO LOGO.** These are the emotional bookends. A mark in the
  corner of 047 turns the most sincere moment in the webinar into an ad. This holds
  even though the logo is genuinely attractive.
- **Clear space** — minimum the cap height of "MOUNTAIN" on all sides.
- **Minimum reproduction** — 180px wide at slide scale. Below that the ™ and the
  "FINANCIAL GROUP, LLC" line stop resolving on a compressed stream.
