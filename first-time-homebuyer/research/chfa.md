# CHFA & Colorado Down Payment Assistance

**Primary sources:** CHFA program materials · county and municipal housing program
pages · employer/professional program administrators
**Used in slide:** 038 · `[EXP-3]` if running early
**Risk level:** 🔴 **HIGHEST IN THE DECK.** Terms, income limits, and *available
funding* change — and some programs exhaust funding mid-year.

---

## The design decision that protects this slide

Slide 038 does **not** teach a list of programs. It teaches **how to check.**

That's deliberate. A list goes stale between deliveries and a stale list is worse
than no list — it sends someone chasing a program that closed. The *procedure*
never goes stale.

**Slide 038 script (verbatim, keep):**
> "Here's my obligation to you: program terms, income limits, and available funding
> change, and some of these run out of money mid-year. Anything specific I told you
> today could be stale by the time you act on it. So the artifact isn't a list of
> programs — it's the instruction: **ask your lender to run the DPA layers for your
> county and your income, on the day you're actually shopping.** A broker can check
> across all of them. Ask by name."

## CHFA — Colorado Housing and Finance Authority

Colorado's state housing finance agency. For first-time buyers, the relevant
offering is:

**A first mortgage** (typically layered over conventional / FHA / VA / USDA)
**+ down payment assistance**, delivered as either:
- a **grant** (no repayment), or
- a **second mortgage** (repayment terms vary by program)

**Generally applicable conditions** 🔴 verify each:
- **Income limits** — by county and household size
- **Purchase price limits** — by county
- **Homebuyer education required** — CHFA-approved course
- **Occupancy** — primary residence
- **Participating lender required** — not every lender is approved. Ask.
- First-time buyer status often required, sometimes with **targeted-area
  exceptions** where it isn't

> The rate on a DPA-paired first mortgage is often slightly higher than a standard
> market first — that's how the assistance is funded. **That tradeoff is exactly
> the kind of thing to run through the breakeven thinking from Module 5.** Sometimes
> the assistance is clearly worth it; sometimes it isn't. Run it.

## ⭐ The four layers — check in this order

This ordering is the actual artifact.

### 1. State — CHFA
Broadest reach. Start here.

### 2. Metro / municipal
Several Front Range municipalities operate their own assistance or affordable
homeownership programs, often with tighter geographic and income targeting — and
sometimes more generous terms than the state program. 🔴 verify current offerings.

### 3. County
Varies widely across Colorado. Some counties have active programs; many don't.
Worth ten minutes of checking, and almost nobody does it.

### 4. Employer / profession / affinity
The most-overlooked layer:
- Teacher, first responder, and healthcare worker programs
- Large-employer housing benefits
- Union and credit-union programs
- Some nonprofit and community-development lenders

> **Delivery line:** *"Nobody ever asks their HR department whether their employer
> has a housing benefit. Some of you have one. It costs you one email to find out."*

## Stacking

**DPA is not an alternative to a loan program — it stacks under the one you chose.**

Slide 039's decision tree picks the *first mortgage*. Then:
> "Whatever the tree says, layer down payment assistance on top. DPA is not a
> program you pick instead. It's a floor you stack under the one you picked."

Compatibility varies by program pairing — some DPA works with FHA and not
conventional, or vice versa. Another reason to have a lender run the layers rather
than have the borrower guess.

## Compliance — read before delivering slide 038

- **Never state a specific income limit, price limit, or assistance amount without
  verifying it for the delivery date.**
- Never imply funding is available — some programs exhaust allocations.
- Never imply eligibility for any individual.
- If CHFA participation status changes for Mountain State Financial Group, the
  slide's framing must change with it. Do not imply access you don't have.
- If time is short and verification wasn't completed: deliver **only** the
  procedure ("ask your lender to run the four layers"). That version is always
  safe and still valuable.
