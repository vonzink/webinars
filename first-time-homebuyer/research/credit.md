## The three corrections this module exists to make

1. The score in your banking app is **not** your mortgage score. (062)
2. Shopping multiple lenders does **not** stack inquiries. (064)
3. The most expensive credit mistake happens **after** approval. (064)

Correction #3 saves the most actual money in the room, because Poll 1 tells you
some attendees are under contract *right now*.

## ⭐ Mortgage scores vs. consumer scores

| | Consumer app score | Mortgage score |
|---|---|---|
| Model | Usually a consumer-education model | Mortgage-specific FICO versions |
| Bureaus | Often one | **All three** |
| Which is used | The one shown | **The MIDDLE of three** — not the average |
| Two borrowers | N/A | Generally the **LOWER** of the two middles |

**Divergence between app scores and mortgage scores can be meaningful** — sometimes
20+ points in either direction. 🟡 Do not state a specific typical gap; say
"they can differ meaningfully, in either direction."

**🔴 VERIFY EACH DELIVERY:** mortgage scoring model requirements have been subject
to an ongoing industry transition. Slide 062's scripted line handles this without
requiring precision:
> "Ask your lender what they're pulling **today**, because the answer has been
> moving."

### The single-borrower calculation
If one borrower's scores are materially lower, the file may price better with only
the stronger borrower on the loan — at the cost of losing the second income for
qualifying. **Real calculation, worth running both ways.** Slide 062 tells the
audience to ask for it.

## Score composition (general FICO model) 🟡 approximate

| Factor | ~Weight | Speed of change |
|---|---|---|
| Payment history | ~35% | Slow — a 30-day late lingers for years |
| Amounts owed / utilization | ~30% | **Fast — 30 to 60 days** |
| Length of credit history | ~15% | Time only |
| New credit / inquiries | ~10% | Fast in, slow out |
| Credit mix | ~10% | Slow |

Present as approximate. Never as exact.

### ⭐ Utilization is the lever
30% of the score, and it can move within one billing cycle. Everything actionable
before a purchase lives here.

- Target **under ~30% per card, ideally under ~10%**
- **Per card matters, not just the aggregate** — one maxed card among five drags you
- Paying a balance before the statement cuts can be more effective than paying
  after, because the reported balance is what scores

### ⭐ Don't close old cards — Poll 4
Closing reduces available credit (raising utilization) and shortens average age.
The "clean up my credit before applying" instinct is right, aimed wrong.

### Disputes
Fix genuine errors — they're more common than people expect. **But an active
dispute flag can stall underwriting.** Fix early, not during the loan. This is
a real, specific, actionable warning that almost nobody gives.

## ⭐ The rate-shopping window

Mortgage inquiries within a shopping window are treated as **one inquiry** for
scoring purposes. **Commonly described as 14 to 45 days depending on the scoring
model.** 🟡

**State it as a range. Never as a guarantee of a specific number of days.**

> **Slide 064 line:** *"The system was deliberately designed so you could shop. Let
> me be blunt about who benefits from you not knowing that. Getting three Loan
> Estimates costs you essentially nothing in score. Not getting them cost that
> woman on slide 53 six thousand four hundred dollars."*

## ⭐ Credit is re-pulled before closing — Story 7

Lenders verify credit again before closing. New debt taken on after approval can
break the file. This is **the single most common preventable disaster** in a
first-time purchase, and almost nobody warns buyers about it.

### The Do-Not-Do List (Artifact #6)
1. No new credit — cards, cars, furniture, phones, buy-now-pay-later
2. Don't close existing accounts
3. No large purchases on existing cards
4. No job changes — especially W-2 → self-employed
5. No moving money without a paper trail
6. No accepting cash gifts without telling your lender first
7. Don't co-sign anything
8. Don't miss a payment on anything

**The covering rule:** *"From application to keys, your financial life is a museum
exhibit. Look, don't touch."*

**The escape valve — say it:** *"If something has to change, that's fine, life
happens. Call your loan officer first. Almost all of these are survivable with
warning and fatal as a surprise."*

## Program minimums 🟡

- **Conventional:** varies by LTV/product; pricing degrades meaningfully below ~660
- **FHA:** 3.5% down at 580+; 10% down in the 500–579 band — **but lender overlays
  are commonly stricter**, and many lenders won't go below 580 or 620 at all
- **VA:** no VA-wide minimum; lender overlays apply
- **USDA:** program guidance plus lender overlays

**Overlays are the reason the same borrower gets different answers at different
lenders.** State this as a fact — it's true, and it happens to be a real argument
for a broker.

## Compliance

- **Never state that a specific score gets a specific rate.** Pricing tiers,
  qualitatively, only.
- Shopping window: always a range, never a guarantee.
- No credit repair advice. Never suggest accurate negative information can be
  removed.
- Direct people to **annualcreditreport.com** — the official free source — not to a
  monitoring product.
- Score factor weights: "approximately."
