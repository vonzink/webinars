# Conventional — Fannie Mae & Freddie Mac

**Primary sources:** Fannie Mae *Selling Guide* · Freddie Mac *Seller/Servicer Guide*
**Cite as:** guide name + topic heading. Section numbers change by edition — do not
cite a section number from memory.
**Used in slides:** 032, 034, 025, 039

---

## Why conventional matters most to this audience

Two reasons, and only the second one usually gets mentioned:

1. Lowest minimum down payment among non-government options (3%).
2. **The mortgage insurance is removable.** This is the structural advantage over
   FHA and it is the single most important comparison point in Module 4.

---

## The 3%-down products

### Conventional 97 (Fannie) / equivalent (Freddie)
- 97% LTV, 30-year fixed
- Purchase, primary residence, 1-unit
- Generally requires at least one borrower to be a first-time homebuyer
- **First-time homebuyer definition:** no ownership interest in a primary
  residence during the **preceding three years**. 🟢
  > Worth saying out loud — people who owned a decade ago assume they're excluded.

### HomeReady (Fannie) / Home Possible (Freddie)
- 3% down
- **Area median income limits apply** 🔴 VERIFY — limits are updated periodically
- **Reduced MI coverage requirement and better loan-level pricing** at the same
  LTV than standard conventional — this is the substantive benefit
- Flexible income: boarder income, accessory unit income, non-occupant
  co-borrowers (program-specific conditions apply)
- **Homebuyer education generally required** — an online course

> **Delivery note:** these are chronically under-mentioned to first-time buyers,
> partly because they're income-limited and partly because they price better,
> which means less revenue on some pricing models. If a borrower is income-eligible
> these are frequently the best product available. Always check.

## DTI

- Commonly to **~45%**; can extend toward **~50%** with strong automated
  underwriting findings 🟡
- **AUS findings govern.** DU (Desktop Underwriter) and LPA (Loan Product Advisor)
  evaluate the whole file — reserves, credit depth, LTV, residual strength — not a
  fixed cap.
- **Never state a DTI cap as a hard rule in the webinar.** Phrasing on slide 025:
  *"commonly go to about 45%, and with strong automated-underwriting findings can
  stretch to 50%."*

## Credit

- Minimum scores vary by LTV/product; **lender overlays are common and often
  stricter than the guide** 🟡
- Pricing adjustments are **tiered**, not linear — the basis for the slide 063
  point that 780→800 usually does nothing while 735→745 can matter
- Two borrowers: the **lower of the two mid scores** generally drives pricing

## Mortgage insurance — the key mechanics

| Event | Threshold | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| Borrower may request cancellation | **80% LTV** | Based on original value; conditions apply (payment history, no junior liens, possible appraisal) |
| Automatic termination | **78% LTV** | By original amortization schedule, primary residence, payments current — **Homeowners Protection Act** 🟢 |
| Final termination | Midpoint of amortization | HPA backstop |
| Appreciation-based | Varies | **Servicer/investor discretion**, seasoning requirements apply — 🔴 not an entitlement |

> **Compliance:** slide 039 must not present appreciation-based removal as a right.
> Scripted as: *"Servicer and investor rules vary. Call your servicer and ask for
> their specific policy in writing."*

## Loan limits

Conforming loan limits are set annually and **vary by county**; some Colorado
counties are high-cost. 🔴 VERIFY current-year limits before delivery.
Not on a slide — Q&A material only.

## Assets

- Gift funds permitted from eligible donors with a gift letter and paper trail
- Large deposits require sourcing
- Reserves may be required depending on the file — AUS-driven

## What to say vs. what not to say

| Say | Don't say |
|---|---|
| "3% down is available on conventional for first-time buyers" | "You'll qualify for 3% down" |
| "DTI commonly goes to about 45%, sometimes 50% with strong findings" | "The DTI limit is 50%" |
| "Conventional MI can be removed; here are the three routes" | "Your PMI comes off at 80%" *(you must request it, and appreciation-based is discretionary)* |
| "If you're income-eligible, ask about HomeReady or Home Possible" | "HomeReady is your best option" |
