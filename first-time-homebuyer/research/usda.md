## Why it's on the slide at all despite being the smallest module

Two corrections that take fifteen seconds each and that a meaningful number of
Colorado buyers need:

1. **"Rural" is not what you think.** Plenty of Colorado towns that feel like
   ordinary towns are eligible. People self-disqualify on vibes.
2. **It's a map, and the map gets updated.** Check the address, not the assumption.

If compressed to one line: *"If you're looking outside the metro core, check the
USDA eligibility map for the address — zero down, and more places qualify than
people think."*

## ⭐ The two income tests people conflate

This trips up borrowers and even some originators:

- **Household income** (eligibility) — counts income of **all adult household
  members**, whether or not they're on the loan. Determines whether you qualify for
  the program at all.
- **Repayment income** (qualifying) — the borrower income used to compute ratios.

A household can be over the eligibility limit while the borrower's repayment income
looks modest. Q&A material — don't put the distinction on a slide, it costs 45
seconds.

---

## Tradeoffs

- **Geographic constraint** is the binding one — it decides where you can live
- **Household income limits** can exclude dual-income households that don't feel
  high-income
- **Property condition standards** can complicate older or distressed housing
- Processing can involve an additional review step, which can affect timelines

---

## Colorado relevance

Genuinely useful outside the Front Range metro core and in many smaller towns and
exurban areas. Worth checking for anyone looking outside the immediate Denver /
Boulder / Colorado Springs cores.

**Do not name specific eligible towns on a slide.** Maps change and a stale example
becomes a wrong one. The slide teaches the *procedure*: check the address.

---

## Compliance

- Never state a specific area is eligible without checking the current map.
- Income limits change — never state a figure without verification.
- Do not imply eligibility for any individual or property.

- [ ] Income limits by household size for target counties
