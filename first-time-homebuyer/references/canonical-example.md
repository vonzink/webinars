# THE CANONICAL EXAMPLE — v5 · single source of truth

> **One house, one loan, all 38 minutes.** Every module adds a layer to the same
> numbers. Never introduce a second property.

**What changed from v4 and why:** the v4 buyers sat at 41.7% DTI — comfortable,
approvable, and dramatically inert. The punchline was that they could buy *more*
house. That's trivia. v5 puts them at **exactly 45.0% — approved at the ceiling** —
so the module can make the claim the whole webinar exists to make: **the system
says yes to a number you should not accept.**

All figures hypothetical, for education only. Not a quote, offer, or commitment to
lend.

## ⭐⭐ THE $105,000 GAP — the spine of the webinar

Comfortable target: **PITI + MI of $2,450** (back-end 36.7%, all-in ≈ $2,850,
a **+$700** step up from rent instead of +$1,483).

**Price factor** (per dollar of purchase price, 95% LTV, 6.500%, CO taxes/ins/MI):

```
0.95 × 0.0063205  (P&I)      = 0.00600448
      × 0.00045833 (taxes)    = 0.00045833
      × 0.00033333 (insurance)= 0.00033333
0.95 × 0.00029167 (MI)        = 0.00027708
                     TOTAL     = 0.00707289 per $1 of price
```

Check: $450,000 × 0.00707289 = **$3,182.80** ✓
Solve: $2,450 ÷ 0.00707289 = **$346,391** → **$345,000**

> # The bank says $450,000.
> # The honest number is $345,000.
> # That's a $105,000 gap.

**And say the uncomfortable part out loud:** in much of the Front Range, $345,000
doesn't buy much. That *is* the actual problem, and pretending otherwise is what
the industry does. Then close the gap — don't leave them there.

---

## ⭐⭐ CLOSING THE GAP — the line that will circulate

Every dollar of monthly debt you remove converts directly into purchase price at
**$1 ÷ 0.00707289 = $141 of house per $1/month.**

| Remove | Buys back |
|---|---|
| **The $415 car payment** | **≈ $59,000 of house** |
| The $290 student loan payment | ≈ $41,000 of house |
| Both | ≈ $100,000 — *nearly the entire gap* |

> **"Your car payment is worth fifty-nine thousand dollars of house."**

That is the most repeatable sentence available to this webinar. It converts
"reduce your debt" from advice into a trade the audience can evaluate on a Saturday
afternoon. It did not exist in v4.

**The five ways to close $105,000** (slide 014):
1. **Kill the car payment** — ≈ $59,000
2. **Down payment assistance** — CHFA / metro / county / employer
3. **Add a co-buyer** — income lands directly in the numerator
4. **Move the search 20 minutes** — the same money buys differently
5. **Eight months** — one credit tier, revolving paid down, more saved
