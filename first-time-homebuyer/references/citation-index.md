## The arithmetic

Every figure in `references/canonical-example.md` and `knowledge/examples.md` must be
**independently re-derived** — not spot-checked — at each fact-check.

This deck's greatest strength is that one example carries all 40 minutes. That's
also its single point of failure: **a wrong P&I figure propagates to the payment
stack, the DTI, the cash to close, the rate table, the breakeven, and the
amortization demo simultaneously.**
