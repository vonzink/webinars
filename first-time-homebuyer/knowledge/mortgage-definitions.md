# Mortgage Definitions

**Rule for the webinar: every term is defined the first time it's spoken, in under
10 words.** The short definition is the one you say out loud. The longer one is
here and in the downloadable glossary.

Marked ⭐ = spoken in the webinar. Everything else is glossary-only.

---

## The payment

**⭐ Principal** — the part of your payment that actually buys the house.
The amount you borrowed, being paid back.

**⭐ Interest** — rent on the money. What the lender charges for lending it.

**⭐ PITI** — Principal, Interest, Taxes, Insurance. The four parts of a typical
mortgage payment. Add MI and HOA for the real total.

**⭐ Escrow (impound) account** — a subscription your lender runs for you. They
collect 1/12 of your annual taxes and insurance monthly, hold it, and pay those
bills when due. Not a fee — your own money, prefunded.

**⭐ Escrow analysis** — the annual recalculation. When taxes or insurance change,
your monthly payment changes. This is why "fixed rate" ≠ "fixed payment."

**⭐ HOA dues** — homeowners association fees. Count against you in underwriting
exactly like a mortgage payment, and can be raised without your vote.

**⭐ Amortization** — the schedule of how each payment splits between interest and
principal. Interest-heavy early, principal-heavy late.

---

## Getting the loan

**⭐ Pre-qualification** — a conversation. Stated numbers, little or no verification.

**⭐ Pre-approval** — credit pulled, documents collected, usually run through
automated underwriting. What sellers expect to see.

**⭐ Underwritten (TBD) approval** — a human underwriter has already reviewed you.
Only the property is left to approve. The strongest thing you can bring to an offer.

**⭐ Underwriting** — the lender's decision process. Verifies income, assets,
credit, and property against program guidelines.

**Automated Underwriting System (AUS)** — Fannie Mae's Desktop Underwriter (DU) or
Freddie Mac's Loan Product Advisor (LPA). Software that issues findings the loan
must then be documented to.

**⭐ Conditions** — the list of items underwriting needs before final approval.
Getting conditions is normal and expected, not a rejection.

**⭐ Clear to Close (CTC)** — all conditions satisfied. Closing can be scheduled.

**Overlay** — a lender's own rule that's stricter than the program requires. Why
the same borrower can be declined at one lender and approved at another — and a
central argument for using a broker who shops multiple lenders.

---

## The money you bring

**⭐ Down payment** — your cash contribution to the purchase price.

**⭐ Closing costs** — fees to *get the loan and transfer the property.* Origination,
appraisal, title, recording. Spent.

**⭐ Prepaids** — costs paid in advance: prepaid interest, the first year of
homeowners insurance. Your own money, front-loaded.

**⭐ Initial escrow deposit** — a few months of taxes and insurance to fund the
escrow account's cushion.

**⭐ Cash to Close** — down payment + closing costs + prepaids + escrows − earnest
money − credits. **The number that matters.** Page 1 of the Loan Estimate.

**⭐ Earnest money** — a good-faith deposit with your offer, held by a neutral
party, credited toward cash to close. Refundable within your contingency deadlines.

**⭐ Seller concessions / seller credits** — the seller pays some of your closing
costs. Program limits apply based on loan type and down payment.

**⭐ Lender credit** — the lender pays some of your costs in exchange for a higher
rate. Points running in reverse.

**⭐ Gift funds** — down payment money from an eligible donor. Requires a gift
letter and a paper trail. Tell your lender *before* it hits your account.

---

## Rate and cost

**⭐ Interest rate** — the rate your payment is calculated from.

**⭐ APR (Annual Percentage Rate)** — the rate plus certain lender costs, expressed
as a yearly rate over the full term. A comparison tool, not your payment rate.

**⭐ Discount points** — prepaid interest to permanently lower your rate. One point
= 1% of the loan amount.

**⭐ Breakeven** — cost of points ÷ monthly savings = months to recover. The only
number that decides whether points are worth it.

**⭐ Temporary buydown (2-1, 3-2-1)** — prefunded money, usually from a seller or
builder, that lowers your effective rate for the first years. **You qualify at the
real rate.**

**⭐ Rate lock** — the lender holds your rate for a set number of days. Extensions
cost money.

**Float-down** — an option some lenders offer to take a lower rate if the market
improves after you lock. Ask before you lock; it's free to ask.

**Par rate** — the rate with no points paid and no lender credit given.

## Ratios and measurements

**⭐ DTI (Debt-to-Income)** — total monthly obligations ÷ gross monthly income.

**⭐ Front-end ratio** — housing payment alone ÷ gross monthly income.

**⭐ Back-end ratio** — housing + all other monthly debts ÷ gross income. The one
that governs.

**⭐ LTV (Loan-to-Value)** — loan amount ÷ property value. 5% down = 95% LTV.

**CLTV** — combined LTV, including second mortgages such as DPA seconds.

**⭐ Residual income** — cash left after all obligations, by family size and region.
**VA's test instead of a hard DTI cap.** Arguably a smarter measure of affordability.

**⭐ Reserves** — liquid assets remaining *after* closing, measured in months of
housing payments.

## Mortgage insurance

**⭐ PMI / BPMI** — borrower-paid monthly mortgage insurance on conventional loans.
**Removable.**

**LPMI** — lender-paid MI, built into a higher rate. Not removable except by
refinancing.

**⭐ FHA MIP** — FHA's mortgage insurance. Upfront + annual. On most FHA loans with
under 10% down, it lasts the **life of the loan**.

**⭐ VA funding fee** — one-time fee, financeable, varies by down payment and prior
use. **Waived** for veterans receiving compensation for a service-connected
disability.

**USDA guarantee fee** — USDA's upfront + annual equivalent.

**⭐ HPA (Homeowners Protection Act)** — federal law requiring automatic PMI
termination at 78% LTV on the original schedule for a primary residence, with
payments current. Request at 80%; automatic at 78%.

---

## Documents

**⭐ Loan Estimate (LE)** — standardized 3-page disclosure, due within 3 business
days of application. **Section A on page 2 is where lenders differ.**

**⭐ Closing Disclosure (CD)** — the final numbers. You must receive it **at least 3
business days before signing.** Compare it to your Loan Estimate.

**Promissory note** — your promise to repay. The debt itself.

**Deed of trust / mortgage** — the security instrument that ties the debt to the
property.

**⭐ Certificate of Eligibility (COE)** — proves VA entitlement.

**⭐ Written List of Providers** — the list of Section C services you're allowed to
shop for. You're handed it; almost nobody uses it.

---

## Property

**⭐ Appraisal** — an independent opinion of value, ordered through a firewall so
neither side can influence it.

**Appraisal gap** — when the appraisal comes in below the contract price. Somebody
has to cover the difference or the deal renegotiates.

**Comparables (comps)** — recent nearby sales used to support value.

**⭐ Contingency** — a contractual right to exit — inspection, appraisal, financing —
within a specific deadline. **The deadline is the whole protection.**

**Title insurance** — protects against defects in ownership history. Lender's policy
protects the lender; owner's policy protects you.

**⭐ Homeowners insurance** — required. Colorado premiums have risen meaningfully
with wildfire and hail exposure. Shop it early — it can affect your qualification.

---

## Programs

**⭐ Conventional** — not government-insured. Follows Fannie Mae or Freddie Mac
guidelines.

**⭐ Conforming loan** — a conventional loan within the annual loan limit for the
county.

**⭐ Conventional 97** — 3% down conventional for first-time buyers.

**⭐ HomeReady / Home Possible** — Fannie/Freddie 3%-down programs with income
limits, reduced MI coverage, and better pricing. Frequently the best first-time
buyer product and chronically under-mentioned.

**⭐ FHA** — government-insured, 3.5% down at 580+, more forgiving credit.

**⭐ VA** — 0% down, no monthly MI, for eligible service members, veterans, and some
surviving spouses. Reusable.

**⭐ USDA** — 0% down in eligible areas with household income limits.

**⭐ CHFA** — Colorado Housing and Finance Authority. First mortgages plus down
payment assistance.

**⭐ DPA (Down Payment Assistance)** — grants or second mortgages that reduce cash
needed. State, metro, county, and employer layers. **Stack it under whatever
program you chose — it isn't an alternative to one.**

**⭐ First-time homebuyer** — most programs: no ownership interest in a primary
residence in the last **three years**. You can have owned before.
