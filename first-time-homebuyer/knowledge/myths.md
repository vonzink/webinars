# Myths

Twelve beliefs the audience walks in with. Each has an **origin** — because every
one of these was true once, or true for someone, and treating them as stupid
insults the room. The frame is always: *you were told this, and nobody corrected it.*

---

## MYTH 1 — "You need 20% down." ⭐
**Truth:** 0% (VA/USDA), 3% (conventional), 3.5% (FHA).
**Origin:** 20% avoids mortgage insurance. Genuinely true. "Avoids one fee" became
"required" somewhere along the way.
**Cost:** The most expensive myth in American homebuying. Story 3, slide 033.

## MYTH 2 — "The Federal Reserve sets mortgage rates." ⭐
**Truth:** The Fed sets an overnight bank rate. 30-year mortgages track the bond
market — mortgage-backed securities, roughly with the 10-year Treasury.
**Origin:** Headlines say "Fed cuts rates" and every consumer rate gets lumped in.
**Proof to cite:** there have been Fed cuts where mortgage rates rose the same day.
**Where:** Slide 041.

## MYTH 3 — "Shopping multiple lenders wrecks your credit." ⭐
**Truth:** Mortgage inquiries inside a shopping window (commonly 14–45 days
depending on the model) count as **one**. The system was built for shopping.
**Origin:** Conflating mortgage inquiries with opening five credit cards.
**Cost:** Story 5. $6,400.
**Where:** Slide 064.

## MYTH 4 — "The lowest interest rate is the best deal." ⭐
**Truth:** Rate ignores points and fees. Loan B's lower rate cost $8,550 upfront
and takes 61 months to pay back.
**Origin:** It's the only number anyone advertises.
**Where:** Slides 044–049.

## MYTH 5 — "Closing costs are what I need at closing."
**Truth:** Cash to close = down payment + closing costs + prepaids + escrows, minus
earnest money and credits. $5,400 vs. $31,760.
**Origin:** Both numbers are on page 1 and the smaller one has the more intuitive
name.
**Where:** Slide 059. Story 6.

## MYTH 6 — "Pre-qualified and pre-approved are the same thing."
**Truth:** Three different documents with very different weight to a seller.
**Origin:** The industry uses the words loosely, sometimes deliberately.
**Where:** Slide 012.

## MYTH 7 — "Closing an unused credit card helps my score." ⭐
**Truth:** Usually hurts — shortens history, removes available credit, raises
utilization.
**Origin:** "Less debt is good" is right; "fewer accounts is good" isn't the same
statement.
**Where:** Poll 4, slide 063.

## MYTH 8 — "I need perfect credit."
**Truth:** Pricing moves in tiers. 735→745 can matter; 780→800 usually doesn't.
FHA goes meaningfully lower than conventional.
**Origin:** Ads that quote a rate footnoted "assumes 780+ FICO."
**Where:** Slide 063.

## MYTH 9 — "Once I'm approved, I'm done."
**Truth:** Credit is re-pulled before closing. Approval stays alive and killable
until you have keys.
**Origin:** Nobody tells buyers this. It's the single most common preventable
disaster.
**Cost:** Story 7. Nine days.
**Where:** Slide 064.

## MYTH 10 — "The builder's incentive is free money."
**Truth:** Often partly funded by rate, fees, or a price that was set to pay for
it. Sometimes it still wins — run the comparison.
**Origin:** It's presented as a discount and it's structured as one.
**Where:** Slide 067. Story 8.

## MYTH 11 — "Renting is throwing money away."
**Truth:** Renting buys flexibility and a capped cost. Over short horizons,
transaction costs often exceed equity built.
**Origin:** A slogan that survived because it sells houses.
**Where:** Slide 004, and `common-objections.md` #4.

## MYTH 12 — "My mortgage payment will never change."
**Truth:** P&I is fixed on a fixed-rate loan. **Taxes, insurance, HOA, and
therefore your total payment are not.** Escrow adjusts, usually annually.
**Origin:** "Fixed rate" gets heard as "fixed payment."
**Where:** Slide 021, reinforced at 023.

## Myths NOT to address (out of scope for a 40-minute class)

Each is real, each would cost 90+ seconds, and none is load-bearing for a
first-time buyer's next decision. Move to Q&A or a follow-up webinar.

- "Biweekly payment programs are magic" — it's just 13 payments; you can do it free
- "You can't buy after a bankruptcy" — you can, after program-specific waiting periods
- "Pre-payment penalties are everywhere" — rare on standard conforming loans now
- "You must use the title company the seller picked" — Section C is shoppable *(this
  one is partly covered on slide 056 — leave it there)*
- "Zillow's estimate is your home's value" — an appraisal is the operative number

## Delivery rule

Never say "that's wrong." Say **"here's where that came from, and here's what
changed."** Every myth in this file was true for somebody. The person who told them
was trying to help.
