# Polls

Five polls. Two are **diagnostic** — the wrong answer is the teaching moment.
Load them in this order in the webinar platform before going live.

**Rule:** never editorialize on a poll result in a way that makes anyone feel
stupid. The reveal targets the industry, never the audience.

---

## POLL 1 — Segmentation
**1:50 · Slide 006 · Single choice · 25 seconds**

> **Where are you right now in the home buying process?**
> - Just curious / thinking about it someday
> - Actively saving, no timeline yet
> - Looking at homes, haven't talked to a lender
> - Working with an agent or lender already
> - Under contract right now

**Purpose:** Segment the room, calibrate depth live, and size the three CTA doors
at the end.

**Live calibration:**
- Weighted early (1–2) → spend more on Modules 3–4, lighter on Module 6
- Weighted late (4–5) → protect Module 6 and the do-not-do list at all costs
- Anyone under contract → *"Stay to minute 31, that segment is specifically for
  you."* This makes one person feel personally seen, and it holds the room.

**Log it.** Compare against Poll 5 and the exit survey.

## POLL 2 — Comfort payment
**12:40 · Slide 028 · Single choice · 20 seconds**

> **What monthly housing payment feels genuinely comfortable to you?**
> (All-in: principal, interest, taxes, insurance, mortgage insurance, HOA)
> - Under $1,800
> - $1,800 – $2,400
> - $2,400 – $3,000
> - $3,000 – $3,600
> - Over $3,600

**Purpose:** Feeds the live Budget Calculator demo — use the modal answer as the
input on slide 027. Also creates a comparison against Exercise 1.

**Script after results:**
> "Now compare that to the number you wrote down at minute eight. Same? Higher?
> For most people the written one is lower — that's your gut, and your gut did
> that before it knew what houses cost. Your gut is usually the honest one."

## POLL 3 — Diagnostic: the down payment myth ⭐
**13:05 · Slide 031 · Single choice · 20 seconds · DO NOT REVEAL IMMEDIATELY**

> **How much do you think you need for a down payment on a $450,000 house?**
> - 3% — $13,500
> - 5% — $22,500
> - 10% — $45,000
> - 20% — $90,000
> - I have no idea

**Purpose:** The single highest-value poll in the deck. Getting the room to commit
to a wrong answer *publicly* is what makes the correction stick — a prediction
error is a far stronger encoder than being told the fact cold.

**Critical:** hold the result on screen without comment. Let it sit. Then advance
to slide 032 and put the actual minimums *next to* the poll bars.

**Script:**
> "So a chunk of this room just told me they need $90,000. The real number, for
> most of you, is somewhere between zero and $15,750."

**Do not say:** "you're all wrong." The frame is always: *you were told this, by
an industry that never corrected it.*

## POLL 4 — Diagnostic: the credit card myth ⭐
**31:00 · Slide 063 · True/False · 15 seconds**

> **True or false: closing a credit card you don't use helps your credit score.**

**Expected:** heavily "true."
**Answer:** **False.** Closing shortens average history and removes available
credit, which raises utilization — the 30% factor. It usually hurts.

**Script:**
> "This one gets almost everybody, and it's genuinely counterintuitive. The
> instinct to 'clean up my credit' before applying is the right instinct pointed
> at the wrong thing. Pay balances down. Don't close accounts."

Fast — 30 seconds total. Don't linger.

## POLL 5 — The success metric ⭐
**39:45 · Slide 070 · Scale 1–10 · 20 seconds**

> **On a scale of 1–10, how confident do you feel about buying a home right now?**

**Purpose:** This is success criterion #1 in `PROJECT.md`. Compare against the
registration-form question (ask the identical question at registration) and the
exit survey. Target: **+3 or better.**

**Script:**
> "Last poll. Same question I'd have asked forty minutes ago — this is how I find
> out whether this worked, and I'll take an honest low number over a polite one."

**Log every delivery.** If the delta shrinks across deliveries, the content has
drifted and needs a re-cut against `versions/v4/CHANGELOG.md`.
