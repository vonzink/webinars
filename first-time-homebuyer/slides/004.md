## Purpose

trading 40 minutes for — and hear that "not yet" is an allowed outcome.

## Speaker notes

> decision.**"

*(Read the five. Read the three. Say them out loud — don't just gesture.)*

> "I want to say that last part clearly, because you will not hear it from many
> people in my job. **For some of you, the right answer today is to keep renting.**
