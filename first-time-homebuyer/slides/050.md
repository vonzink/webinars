# Slide 050

**Module:** 5 · **Time:** 23:10 · **Duration:** 35s · **Type:** Data · `[CUT-4]`

## Purpose
Explain temporary buydowns honestly — the real benefit and the real trap — and set
up Module 8's builder analysis.

## Headline
**A temporary buydown is not the same as points.**

## On-slide content
**2-1 buydown:** year 1 acts ~2% lower · year 2 ~1% lower · year 3+ the real rate
Prefunded into escrow — **usually paid by the seller or builder**

**The good:** real cash-flow relief in the two most expensive years, often on
somebody else's money. Unused funds are generally credited toward your loan.

**The trap:** **you qualify at the REAL rate.** You will be paying the full payment
in year three.

> **A price reduction is permanent. A temporary buydown is two years.**
> **Ask which one you can have. Sometimes you can have either.**

## Speaker notes
> "Different instrument. Not the same as points."

> "A **2-1 buydown**: year one your rate acts about two percent lower, year two
> about one percent lower, year three onward it's the real rate. The difference is
> prefunded into an escrow account — and **usually paid by the seller or the builder
> as a concession.**"

> "The good: real cash-flow relief in the two most expensive years of ownership,
> often on somebody else's money. And if you refinance or sell and there are unused
> funds left, those are generally credited toward your loan rather than lost."

*(Beat. Point at year three.)*

> "The trap: **you qualify at the real rate, not the bought-down rate.** You will be
> paying that full payment in year three. If the plan depends on refinancing before
> then, that plan depends on rates you cannot control."

*(Beat.)*

> "And the sharper version, which matters in about ten minutes. When a **builder**
> offers this, ask what it's costing you. Sometimes a rate buydown is funded by a
> price that was set high enough to pay for it. **A price reduction is permanent. A
> temporary buydown is two years.** Ask which one you can have. Sometimes you can
> have either."

`[CUT-4]` **compressed:** *"You'll hear about 2-1 buydowns, usually from builders.
The thing to know: you qualify at the real rate, and a price cut is permanent while
a buydown is two years. Ask me in Q&A."* Saves 35s.
