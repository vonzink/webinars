# Slide 013

**Module:** 2 · **Time:** 5:15 · **Duration:** 35s · **Type:** Build

## Purpose
Hand over the most portable question in the entire webinar: *"Who pays you, and
how?"* Seeds mistake #9.

## Headline
**Seven people. Two of them work for you.**

## On-slide content

| Who | Works for | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| Your agent | **You** (per your buyer agreement) | Read how they're paid. Ask. |
| Listing agent | **The seller** | Lovely. Not yours. |
| Loan officer | You (broker) or the lender (retail) | Ask which. It changes incentives. |
| Processor | The lender | Your document conduit. Be kind. |
| Underwriter | The lender/investor | Never speaks to you. Decides everything. |
| Appraiser | Independent, firewalled | Neither side can lean on them. |
| Title / escrow | Neutral | Handles the money and the deed. |

## Graphic
Seven avatars arranged in a ring around the buyer. On each click, one avatar turns
to face whoever it works for. The listing agent visibly turns *away*.

## Animation
Rows reveal with the avatar turns. Final click surfaces the closing question.

## Speaker notes
> "Seven people in your transaction, and they do not all work for you."

*(Move fast — name each, one line.)*

> "The listing agent is going to be lovely to you. They are also legally not yours.
> The appraiser is deliberately walled off so neither side can lean on them —
> that's a feature. The underwriter decides everything and you will never speak to
> them."

*(Beat.)*

> "And here's maybe the most useful sentence in this whole webinar: **'Who pays
> you, and how?'** Ask everybody. Nobody good is offended by that question."

> "That's **mistake number nine** — not asking. Hold onto it."

*(Chat prompt: "Anyone already working with an agent? Drop a 👋")*
