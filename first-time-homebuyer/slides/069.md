# Slide 069

**Module:** 9 · **Time:** 37:15 · **Duration:** 75s · **Type:** Recap → Emotional beat

## Purpose
Finish the recap, then deliver the emotional peak of the webinar — naming the
single thing that produced every story told today.

## Headline
**6 through 9. And the one that costs the most.**

Then, alone on black:
**"Not asking questions because you're afraid of looking like you don't know what
you're doing."**

## On-slide content
**6.** Waiting years to reach 20% down
**7.** Buying at the very top of your approval
**8.** Taking the builder incentive without comparing
**9.** Never asking who gets paid, and how

Then — **clear everything.**

## Graphic
Four numbered rows with story thumbnails, matching slide 068.

**Then the slide clears completely to near-black `#0B1218`** and one line of white
text appears alone. **No logo. No footer. No progress indicator. No branding of any
kind.** This is one of the three emotional-beat slides.

## Animation
Four rows, fast. Then a **full clear to black** — a hard cut, not a fade. Two-second
hold on empty black. Then the line fades in over 800ms.

## Speaker notes
> "**Six — waiting years to reach twenty percent.** Tom and Ashley. Six years
> avoiding a temporary fee. Run the comparison — then decide. Just don't let it be
> a default."

> "**Seven — buying at the very top of your approval.** The bank asks whether you'll
> repay. It never asks whether you'll be okay."

> "**Eight — taking the builder incentive without comparing.** Sometimes it wins.
> You just have to actually check. It took him ten minutes."

> "**Nine — never asking who gets paid, and how.** Ask everybody. Nobody good is
> offended by that question."

*(Beat. **Now change everything — drop the pace completely. The tempo change is what
makes this land.**)*

> "And the most expensive one."

*(Full clear to black. **Two seconds of silence on an empty slide.** Then the line
fades in.)*

> "**Not asking questions because you're afraid of looking like you don't know what
> you're doing.**"

*(Pause.)*

> "Every single story I told you today started there. Marcus and Dani didn't ask
> what the first step was. The guy who served four years never asked if it counted —
> and almost paid tens of thousands of dollars for the silence. The woman with two
> Loan Estimates didn't ask what Section A was. The sofa guy didn't ask if he could
> buy furniture."

> "None of those people were uninformed because they were careless. They were
> uninformed because **asking felt embarrassing** — and this industry has been
> pretty comfortable with that, because a client who doesn't ask questions is a
> faster close."

*(Beat.)*

> "So here's the last thing I'll say about mistakes. **You are allowed to not know
> this.** You've done this zero times. The people across the table have done it
> thousands. That asymmetry isn't a character flaw, it's just arithmetic — and the
> only thing that closes it is asking."

> "Every question you were embarrassed to ask today, somebody else in this room
> typed into the chat."

*(**Let the silence run two full seconds before advancing.** Do not step on it.)*

## Transition
> "Which brings us to the last two minutes. And I want to use them to make sure you
> leave with something in your hands — not just something in your head."
