# Slide 003

**Module:** 1 · **Time:** 0:45 · **Duration:** 20s · **Type:** Build

## Purpose
Name the shared experience of being talked at in jargon — so the audience feels
recognized rather than deficient. Shifts blame from them to the industry.

## Headline
**You've been sold to. You haven't been taught.**

## On-slide content
Acronym storm: `APR` `LTV` `DTI` `PITI` `MIP` `LLPA` `TRID` `AUS` `BPMI` `UFMIP`
`CLTV` `COE` — then all clear except one word: **"why?"**

## Graphic
Twelve acronyms scattered across the slide, different sizes and angles, animating
in fast and overlapping until the slide is genuinely unreadable. Then everything
fades except a single centered **"why?"** in Alpenglow.

## Animation
1. Acronyms animate in over ~2 seconds, accelerating, overlapping
2. Hold the unreadable state for one full beat
3. All fade to 0 except **"why?"**

The moment of unreadability is the point. Don't shorten it.

## Speaker notes
> "The mortgage industry has a habit. It explains just enough to make you feel like
> you need us, and stops right before the part that would let you check our work."

> "Every one of you has had a version of this conversation. Somebody throws six
> acronyms at you, you nod — because what else are you going to do — and then you
> go home and Google them alone at eleven at night."

> "Two things are true. Mortgages are genuinely complicated. And they are also
> deliberately over-complicated. Today we're only dealing with the first kind."

## Transition
> "So here's what you're getting."
