## Speaker notes
**Full run-book: `resources/budget-demo.md`. Rehearse from that file, not this one.**

> "Every calculator online does this forwards: here's a price, here's a payment.
> Mine does it backwards, because backwards is the direction you actually think.
> You don't wake up wondering what a four-fifty house costs. You wake up wondering
> what you can handle."

*(Type the modal answer from Poll 2.)*

> "Poll said most of you are around [X]. Let's put that in. There it is — at [X] a
> month, all-in, you're looking at about [PRICE]."

*(Flip the "real ownership costs" toggle.)*

> "Now watch. This adds the maintenance reserve, the utility difference, the
> furnishing budget — the stuff we just talked about."

*(Price drops.)*

> "Look at that. Same comfort level. About **sixty thousand dollars less house.**
> And that's the honest version. Every calculator that leaves that off is quietly
> telling you that you can afford more than you can. It's not malicious — 'here's a
> bigger number' is just a better user experience, right up until you own the
> house."
