# Slide 042

**Module:** 5 · **Time:** 18:45 · **Duration:** 35s · **Type:** Build

## Purpose
Explain why "what's your rate" is unanswerable — which is simultaneously the
teaching point and the compliance-safe response to the question that *will* appear
in chat.

## Headline
**Seven things change your rate. That's why "what's your rate" is unanswerable.**

## On-slide content
1. **Credit score** — biggest lever, and it moves in **tiers**
2. **Loan-to-value** — your down payment
3. **Loan program** — conventional / FHA / VA / USDA price differently
4. **Occupancy** — primary vs. second home vs. investment
5. **Property type** — single-family vs. condo vs. 2–4 unit
6. **Loan term** — 15-year prices below 30-year
7. **Points or credits** — buying the rate down, or taking it up for cash

## Graphic
Seven dials arranged around a center "YOUR RATE" readout. As each is named, the
dial turns and the center number visibly moves.

## Animation
One dial per click. The center number shifting each time is the whole idea.

## Speaker notes
> "The market sets a base. Then your file adjusts it. Seven things."

*(Fast — name each with one clause.)*

> "Credit score — the biggest single lever, and it moves in **tiers**, not smoothly.
> Loan-to-value. Program. Occupancy — primary versus second home versus investment,
> and those are big jumps. Property type. Term — a fifteen prices below a thirty.
> And points."

*(Beat.)*

> "Which is why 'what's your rate today' is an unanswerable question. Not evasive —
> **unanswerable.**"

> "It's like asking a doctor 'what's the dose.' Dose of what, for whom? Anyone who
> fires back a number without knowing five of those seven things is quoting you a
> rate you may not qualify for. **That's not a quote. That's bait.**"
