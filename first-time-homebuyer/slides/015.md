## Headline
**Five things that kill deals after contract.**

## On-slide content
1. **New debt** — the car, the furniture, the credit card
2. **Job change** — especially W-2 → self-employed
3. **Large unexplained deposits** — every dollar needs a story
4. **A low appraisal** — the gap has to come from somewhere
5. **Missed contract deadlines** — those are real dates

## Speaker notes
> "Five things. Fast."

> "**New debt** — the car, the furniture, the credit card. That's **mistake number
> four**, and it has its own segment at minute thirty-one because it's the most
> common preventable disaster in this entire business."

> "**Job change** — especially going from a W-2 to self-employed. Congratulations,
> and please close first."

> "**Large unexplained deposits.** Every dollar has to have a story. If your mom is
> helping you, that's completely fine — tell your lender *before* the money lands."

> "**Low appraisal** — somebody has to cover the gap."

> "**Missed deadlines.** Your inspection date and your financing date are
> contractual dates, not suggestions. Blow one and your earnest money can be at
> risk."
