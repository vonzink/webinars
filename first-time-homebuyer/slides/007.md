# Slide 007

**Module:** 1 · **Time:** 2:15 · **Duration:** 15s · **Type:** Build

## Purpose
Give the audience a spatial model of the next 38 minutes so they always know where
they are. Reduces the low-grade anxiety of not knowing how long something will last.

## Headline
**Ten stops. Here's the whole trip.**

## On-slide content
1. The process, end to end
2. What you can actually afford
3. Down payment & programs
4. Rates, APR & buydowns
5. The Loan Estimate
6. Credit
7. Builder vs. existing
8. The 9 expensive mistakes
9. Homework & downloads
10. Your questions

## Graphic
**Hero asset.** A horizontal journey line with 10 stops. This graphic is reused as
the **footer progress indicator on every section slide for the rest of the deck** —
build it once, well. Spec in `diagrams/`.

## Animation
The line draws left to right over ~1.5s, stops appearing as it passes them.
