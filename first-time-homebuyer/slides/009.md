## Speaker notes
> "Marcus and Dani spent five months looking. Every weekend. They knew the school
> boundaries, they knew which streets got afternoon sun, they had opinions about
> countertops that I did not understand."

> "They walked into a house on a Saturday and both of them just — stopped. It was
> the one. They called their agent from the driveway. Agent says, great, let's
> write it up — send me your pre-approval letter."

*(Beat.)*

> "They didn't have one. They'd been meaning to. It was on the list."

> "It took them six days to get one. The house went under contract in—"

**[CLICK]**

> "—**six hours.** To somebody who'd done the boring part first."

*(Beat.)*

> "Here's what I want you to take from that. They didn't lose that house because
> they had less money. They had **more** money than the buyer who got it. They lost
> it because they did step four before step one."

## Transition
> "So let's fix the order. Here's the whole thing on one slide."
