# Slide 014

**Module:** 2 · **Time:** 5:50 · **Duration:** 35s · **Type:** Data

## Purpose
Normalize the anxious middle of the transaction so it doesn't get misread as
something going wrong.

## Headline
**Contract to keys: 30 to 45 days.**

## On-slide content
- **Day 0** — Under contract. Earnest money deposited.
- **Day 1–3** — Application. **Loan Estimate within 3 business days.**
- **Day 3–10** — Inspection window. *Deadlines are contractual dates.*
- **Day 5–15** — Appraisal ordered and completed
- **Day 10–25** — Underwriting → **conditions.** *(the anxious part)*
- **Day 25–30** — Clear to close
- **Day 27–30** — **Closing Disclosure at least 3 business days before signing**
- **Day 30** — Sign, fund, record, keys

## Animation
Ranges shade in sequence as spoken.

## Speaker notes
> "Thirty to forty-five days is typical from contract to keys."

*(Walk it fast.)*

> "The part nobody warns you about is the middle. Days ten through twenty-five,
> you're going to get emails asking for things you already sent. It feels like they
> think you're lying to them."

*(Beat.)*

> "They don't. Underwriting is a checklist, and the checklist doesn't know what you
> already did. Send it again, same day, and it's a non-event. Send it in four days
> and it delays your closing. That's genuinely the whole difference."

> "Two dates on here are federal protections, not courtesies. Loan Estimate within
> three business days of applying. Closing Disclosure at least three business days
> before you sign. That second one exists so you have time to actually read it."
