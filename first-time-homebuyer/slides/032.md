# Slide 032

**Module:** 4 · **Time:** 13:20 · **Duration:** 40s · **Type:** Data · **HERO REVEAL** ⭐

## Purpose
Demolish the 20% myth against the audience's own just-committed guesses. The
highest-value 40 seconds in the webinar.

## Headline
**20% is a myth with a body count.**

## On-slide content

| Program | Minimum down | On $450,000 |
|---|---|---|
| **VA** (eligible service) | **0%** | **$0** |
| **USDA** (eligible area & income) | **0%** | **$0** |
| **Conventional** (Conv 97 / HomeReady / Home Possible) | **3%** | **$13,500** |
| **FHA** | **3.5%** | **$15,750** |
| Conventional standard | 5% | $22,500 |

> **Where 20% came from:** at 20% down you avoid mortgage insurance.
> "Avoids one fee" became "required."

*Program minimums. Eligibility depends on credit, income, property, and lender
requirements.*

## Speaker notes

> "So — a chunk of this room just told me they need ninety thousand dollars."

*(Beat. Then reveal.)*

> "The real number, for most of you, is somewhere between **zero and fifteen
> thousand seven hundred fifty.**"

*(Walk the table.)*

> "VA — zero down. USDA — zero down. Conventional — three percent, thirteen five.

*(Beat.)*

> "Now — where did twenty percent come from? Because it's not made up. **At twenty
> percent down you avoid mortgage insurance.** That's a real benefit, and that's
