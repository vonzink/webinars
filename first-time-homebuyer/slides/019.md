# Slide 019

**Module:** 3 · **Time:** 7:50 · **Duration:** 30s · **Type:** Emotional beat / Exercise

## Purpose
Get a number committed to paper **before** any math can rationalize it upward. The
gut number is the honest one and it must be captured first.

## Headline
**Write it down. Right now.**

## On-slide content
> The highest monthly housing payment you could pay **every single month for ten
> years** — and still take the trip, still fix the car, still say yes to dinner.

*Don't compute anything. Gut number.*

**[ 00:15 ]**

## Graphic
**Near-black `#0B1218`.** White text. A live 15-second countdown. **No logo, no
footer, no branding, no progress indicator.** Nothing else on the slide.

## Animation
The timer runs. That's the entire animation.

## Speaker notes
> "Everybody, right now. Piece of paper, phone notes, back of an envelope."

> "Write down the highest monthly housing payment you could pay **every single
> month for the next ten years** — and still take the trip, still fix the car,
> still say yes when somebody asks you to dinner."

> "Don't compute anything. Don't look anything up. Gut number. Write it down and
> hold onto it — we're coming back to it in five minutes."

*(**Let the full 15 seconds run.** Say nothing. It will feel long. The silence is
the exercise — if you fill it, people stop writing and start listening, and the
whole point was to make them commit before they could think.)*

## Transition
> "Hold that number. Now let me show you why it matters more than the one on your
> pre-approval letter."
