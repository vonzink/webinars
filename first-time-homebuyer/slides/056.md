# Slide 056

**Module:** 6 · **Time:** 26:25 · **Duration:** 45s · **Type:** Data · **HERO** · `[EXP-2]`

## Purpose
Teach the single most valuable skill in the webinar: **A and C.** Everything else on
page 2 is noise for comparison purposes.

## Headline
**Two letters. A and C.**

## On-slide content

| Section | What it is | Shoppable? |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Origination Charges** | The lender's own fees + points | **No — but shop lenders. THIS IS THE BOX.** |
| **B. Services You Cannot Shop For** | Appraisal, credit report, flood cert | No |
| **C. Services You CAN Shop For** | Title, settlement, survey, pest | **YES — and almost nobody does** |
| E. Taxes & Government Fees | Recording, transfer taxes | No |
| F. Prepaids | Prepaid interest, first-year insurance | Not really |
| G. Initial Escrow Deposit | The servicer's cushion | No |

> *Section A is the **labor line** on a repair estimate. Parts cost what parts cost.
> Labor is where shops differ.*

## Graphic
**HERO.** Page 2 of the sample form. **Sections A and C in full color. Everything
else desaturated to grey.** That single visual choice teaches the whole lesson.

## Animation
Full page in grey. A colors in. C colors in. Nothing else changes.

## Speaker notes
> "Page two is where lenders actually differ. And there are only two boxes that
> matter for comparison."

> "**Section A — Origination Charges.** The lender's own fees, plus any points.
> **This is the box.** This is where Section A was thirteen hundred at one lender
> and seven thousand seven hundred at another, on the same rate."

> "**Section B — services you cannot shop for.** Appraisal, credit report, flood
> certification. Everybody pays roughly the same. Not a lever."

> "**Section C — services you CAN shop for.** Title, settlement, survey, pest. And
> here's the leverage nobody uses: **the lender has to hand you a Written List of
> Providers, and you are allowed to go get your own.** Title and settlement fees can
> vary by four figures between companies in the same city. **Almost nobody shops
> it.**"

> "E through G — recording fees, prepaids, escrow deposit — those are mostly not the
> lender's doing. Your property taxes are your property taxes. Nobody's competing
> on those."

*(Beat.)*

> "So the mental model: **Section A is the labor line on a repair estimate.** Parts
> cost what parts cost. Labor is where shops differ."
