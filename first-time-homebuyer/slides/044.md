# Slide 044

**Module:** 5 · **Time:** 19:55 · **Duration:** 30s · **Type:** Statement

## Purpose
Define APR in plain language and explain *why it exists* — the "why" is what makes
it memorable.

## Headline
**APR isn't your interest rate.**

## On-slide content
**Interest rate** — what your payment is calculated from
**APR** — the rate **plus certain lender costs**, spread across the full term,
expressed as a yearly rate

> The rate is the **sticker price.** APR is closer to the **out-the-door price.**

*APR exists because lenders learned you'd shop on rate alone.*

## Graphic
A car windshield sticker showing one number, next to a final invoice showing a
larger one — with a visible block of fees stapled to the back page.

## Animation
Sticker first. Invoice on click, fees highlighting in Clay.

## Speaker notes
> "Two numbers on every mortgage disclosure. They are never the same, and almost
> nobody can tell you why."

> "**Interest rate** — that's what your payment is calculated from. **APR** — that's
> the rate plus certain lender costs, spread across the full loan term, expressed
> as a yearly rate."

*(Beat.)*

> "The analogy: the **rate is the sticker price on the car. The APR is closer to the
> out-the-door price.** Two dealers can advertise the exact same sticker and hand
> you very different final numbers, because one of them has six thousand dollars of
> fees stapled to the back page."

> "APR exists because lenders figured out you'd shop on rate alone — so they'd
> advertise a beautiful rate and make it up in fees. APR was the regulatory
> response. It's imperfect. It is still the best single comparison number you have."

## Transition
> "So let's put two real loans next to each other and you tell me which one's
> better."
