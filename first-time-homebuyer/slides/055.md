# Slide 055

**Module:** 6 · **Time:** 25:45 · **Duration:** 40s · **Type:** Build

## Purpose
Orient the audience on page 1 and flag the two-numbers problem that becomes Story 6.

## Headline
**Page 1: five boxes.**

## On-slide content
1. **Loan Terms** — amount, rate, P&I, and yes/no columns: *can this increase?*
   prepayment penalty? balloon?
2. **Projected Payments** — payment over time, including MI and when it drops
3. **Estimated Taxes, Insurance & Assessments** — the escrow line
4. **Costs at Closing** — ⚠️ **two different numbers:** *Closing Costs* and
   *Cash to Close*
5. **Rate lock** — is it locked, and until when

> **Compare Loan Estimates issued the SAME DAY. Otherwise you're comparing
> weather.**

## Graphic
Page 1 of the sample form, with the five boxes numbered and highlighting in
sequence. Box 4 gets an Alpenglow flag.

## Animation
One box highlights per click. Box 4's flag is the last beat.

## Speaker notes
> "Page one. Five boxes."

> "**Loan Terms** — amount, rate, monthly principal and interest. And a set of
> yes/no columns: *can this increase after closing?* Prepayment penalty? Balloon
> payment? On a standard thirty-year fixed those should all be the boring answer.
> **If any of them surprise you, stop and ask why.**"

> "**Projected Payments** — your payment over time, including mortgage insurance and
> when it comes off."

> "**Estimated taxes, insurance and assessments** — the escrow line."

> "**Costs at Closing** — and pay attention here, because there are **two different
> numbers in this box.** Estimated Closing Costs, and Estimated Cash to Close. Those
> are not the same thing and we're going to come back to it in about three minutes."

> "**Rate lock** — is this locked, and until when."

*(Beat.)*

> "One rule before we go to page two: **if you compare two Loan Estimates issued on
> different days, you're not comparing lenders. You're comparing days.** Rates move.
> Same day. Ask for it."

## Transition
> "Page two. This is the one."
