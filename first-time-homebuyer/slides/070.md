# Slide 070

**Module:** 10 — Close · **Time:** 38:30 · **Duration:** 90s · **Type:** Build

## Purpose
Convert understanding into action. **Value first, unconditionally. Then the ask.**

## Headline
**Homework. Downloads. Three doors.**

## On-slide content

**HOMEWORK — 7 things**
1. Pull your credit — free, official federal site. Look for errors.
2. Run the **Payment-Shock Test** — 3 months
3. Write down your **Number** and keep it somewhere you'll see it
4. **Call one lender.** Not to apply. Twenty minutes.
5. List your debts — balance, minimum, rate
6. Ask someone to run the **DPA layers** for your county and income
7. If you or your spouse served — **get your COE**

**DOWNLOADS — 5 files, no form, no email**
First-Time Buyer Checklist · Closing Cost Worksheet · Loan Estimate Cheat Sheet ·
Questions to Ask a Builder · Mortgage Glossary

**THREE DOORS**
🚪 **1 — Nothing.** Take the kit and go.
🚪 **2 — Send me a Loan Estimate. Any lender. Free. No strings.** ⭐
🚪 **3 — A 20-minute Numbers Call.** No credit pull. No application.

**POLL 5** — confidence, 1–10

## Speaker notes

**HOMEWORK (20s)**
> "Seven things. If you do the first three this week you'll be further along than
> most people are three months in."

*(Read them fast.)*

**DOWNLOADS (15s)**
> "Five things, linked in the chat right now. **No form. No email required.** If you
> came for these and you leave right now, that is a completely fine outcome."

**THE THREE DOORS (40s)**
> "And then, if you want anything from me — three doors, sized differently on
> purpose, because Poll one told me this room is in about five different places."

> "**Door one — nothing.** Genuinely. You now know more than most buyers do at
> closing. That was the point. If I never hear from you, this still worked."

> "**Door two — and this is the one I actually want you to take.** If you have a
> Loan Estimate from **any** lender — mine, theirs, the builder's, the bank your
> parents use — **send it to me. I will mark it up and send it back.** Section A
> circled. Points identified. Cash to close explained. What I'd ask them. No cost,
> no obligation, and **I don't care whether I'm your lender.**"

> "I'll tell you exactly why, because 'free' should always make you suspicious. Two
> reasons. One — if you send me a good Loan Estimate and I tell you it's good,
> you'll remember who told you the truth when it cost them something. Two — I've
> seen enough bad ones that I'd rather do this than watch another first-time buyer
> overpay by four figures because nobody read page two."

> "**That's the whole pitch. That's the most aggressive thing I'm going to do
> today.**"

> "**Door three — a twenty-minute Numbers Call.** No credit pull. No application. No
> commitment. You tell me income, debts, savings; I tell you what's actually
> possible, which programs fit, and what 'not yet' would look like if that's the
> honest answer. And if the answer is 'keep renting for eight months and here's
> why,' I'll say that. I've said it plenty of times and I'd rather say it now than
> after you're in escrow."

*(**Say the email address out loud** — replay viewers can't click chat.)*

**POLL 5 (15s)**
> "Last poll. **On a scale of one to ten, how confident do you feel about buying a
> home right now?** Same question I'd have asked forty minutes ago — this is how I
> find out whether this worked, and I'll take an honest low number over a polite
> one."

**CLOSE**
> "Last thing. You came in today worried about making an expensive mistake. That
> worry was **appropriate** — this is a lot of money and the stakes are real."

> "But the expensive mistake was never going to be picking the wrong rate by an
> eighth of a point. It was going to be **signing something you didn't understand
> because you were embarrassed to ask.**"

> "You can't make that one anymore. You know where Section A is. You know what cash
> to close means. You know your DTI, you know the breakeven question, and you know
> that 'no' is almost never the answer."

*(Beat.)*

> "That's not nothing. **That's the whole thing.**"

> "Q&A is open. I'll stay as long as there are questions."
