# Slide 062

**Module:** 7 · **Time:** 30:05 · **Duration:** 45s · **Type:** Data

## Purpose
Correct the belief that the app score is the mortgage score, and hand over the
single-borrower calculation nobody knows to ask for.

## Headline
**The score in your app is not your mortgage score.**

## On-slide content

| | Your banking app | **Mortgage** |
|---|---|---|
| Model | Consumer-education model | Mortgage-specific FICO versions |
| Bureaus | Often one | **All three** |
| Which is used | The one shown | **The MIDDLE of three** — not the average |
| Two borrowers | — | Generally the **LOWER** of the two middles |

Maya 742 · Dev 758 → **priced off 742**

> **Ask your lender what they're pulling today. The answer has been moving.**

## Speaker notes
> "Show of hands in the chat — who knows their credit score?"

*(Wait. Lots of yes.)*

> "Okay. **Almost none of you know your mortgage credit score.**"

> "Three things nobody tells you."

> "**One — different model.** The score in your banking app is usually a
> consumer-education model. Mortgage lending has historically used older,
> mortgage-specific FICO versions. They can differ meaningfully — sometimes twenty
> points or more, in either direction."

> "**Two — three bureaus, and they use the middle one.** Equifax, Experian,
> TransUnion hold different data. Your lender pulls all three and uses the
> **middle** score. Not the average. The middle."

> "**Three — two borrowers?** Generally the **lower** of the two middle scores
> drives your pricing."

*(Beat.)*

> "So Maya's a 742, Dev's a 758 — they get priced off **742.**"

> "Which leads somewhere people find uncomfortable: **sometimes a couple qualifies
> for a better loan with only one borrower on it.** Not always — you lose the second
> income too. But it's a real calculation and your lender should run it both ways.
> **Ask for it.**"

> "One more: the scoring model is changing. There's been an ongoing industry
> transition. **Ask your lender what they're pulling today**, because the answer has
> been moving."
