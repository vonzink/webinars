## Purpose

## Headline

## On-slide content
**0% down** — in USDA-eligible areas, under the household income limit

Two things people get wrong:
1. **"Rural" doesn't mean a farm.** Plenty of Colorado towns qualify.
2. **It's a map, and it gets updated.** Check the address, not the vibe.

Tradeoffs: upfront + annual fee · **household** income limits (everyone in the
house) · property condition standards
