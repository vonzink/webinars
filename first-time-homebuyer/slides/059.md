## Speaker notes
> "A buyer told me, very confidently, that his closing costs were fifty-four
> hundred dollars. He was right. It was on page one. He'd read it."

*(Beat.)*

> "He showed up at the title company with a cashier's check and was **four thousand
> one hundred dollars short.** In the parking lot. Calling his brother."

*(Click — the big number.)*

> "Down payment, twenty-two five. Closing costs, fifty-four hundred. Prepaid
> interest, about eleven forty-two. A year of homeowners insurance, eighteen
> hundred. Escrow reserves, nine eighteen."

> "**Cash to close: thirty-one thousand seven hundred sixty dollars.** Minus his
> earnest money — twenty-six seven sixty due at the table."

*(Beat.)*

> "Closing costs: fifty-four hundred. Cash to close: thirty-one seven sixty.
> **Those are not the same word, and they are twenty-six thousand dollars apart.**"

> "That's **mistake number five.** The number you plan your life around is **Cash to
> Close. Page one. Bottom right.** Not Closing Costs. If you remember one physical
> location on one piece of paper from today, make it that one."
