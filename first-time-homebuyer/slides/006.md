# Slide 006

**Module:** 1 · **Time:** 1:50 · **Duration:** 25s · **Type:** Poll

## Purpose
Segment the room, calibrate depth live, and establish that participation is
expected — the first ask is deliberately the cheapest one of the whole webinar.

## Headline
**Where are you right now?**

## On-slide content
**POLL 1**
- Just curious / thinking about it someday
- Actively saving, no timeline yet
- Looking at homes, haven't talked to a lender
- Working with an agent or lender already
- Under contract right now

## Graphic
Poll overlay from the webinar platform. Deck dims to 30% behind it.

## Animation
Platform-native.

## Speaker notes
> "Before we start — I want to know who's in the room, because it changes what I
> emphasize. Five options, pick the closest."

*(Give it 20 seconds. Read the results aloud — not everyone can see them.)*

**Live calibration:**
- Weighted to 1–2 → lean into Modules 3–4, lighter on Module 6
- Weighted to 4–5 → protect Module 6 and the do-not-do list at all costs
- **Anyone under contract:** *"Whoever's under contract — stay to minute
  thirty-one. That segment is specifically for you and it might save you the
  closing."*

That last line makes one person feel personally seen and holds the whole room.

## Transition
