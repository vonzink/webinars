# Slide 020

**Module:** 3 · **Time:** 8:20 · **Duration:** 30s · **Type:** Data

## Purpose
Make explicit that two different budgets exist and only one of them is anybody
else's job to compute.

## Headline
**Two budgets. Only one of them is about your life.**

## On-slide content

| | **The bank's budget** | **Your budget** |
|---|---|---|
| Question it answers | "Will you repay?" | **"Will you be okay?"** |
| Looks at | Gross income, documented debt | Take-home, real life |
| Ignores | Childcare, groceries, retirement, travel, pets, the dog's surgery | Nothing |
| Output | Your **maximum** | Your **comfortable** |

## Graphic
Split screen. Left: cold, institutional, Ridge Blue, sterile. Right: warm, Snow,
Alpenglow accent. The visual temperature difference does half the work.

## Animation
Rows reveal together across both columns.

## Speaker notes
> "There are two budgets in this transaction and people conflate them constantly."

> "The bank's budget asks: will you repay? It looks at gross income and documented
> debt, and it ignores childcare, groceries, retirement, travel, pets, and the fact
> that your dog is nine."

> "Your budget asks: will you be **okay**? It ignores nothing."

*(Beat.)*

> "One of these is printed on your pre-approval letter. The other one is your actual
> life. **Nobody in the transaction is going to compute the second one for you.**
> Today you're going to."

## Transition
> "So let's build a payment from scratch, because most people don't actually know
> what's in one."
