# Slide 005

**Module:** 1 · **Time:** 1:30 · **Duration:** 20s · **Type:** Statement

## Purpose
**The single highest-leverage slide in the first five minutes.** Removes the brace
the audience walked in with. Everything after this is heard differently.

## Headline
**Five things I'm not going to do.**

## On-slide content
1. ~~Quote you a rate~~
2. ~~Tell you rates are about to go up~~
3. ~~Use a countdown, a deadline, or "this expires"~~
4. ~~Make you give me your email to get anything~~
5. ~~Pretend buying is always right~~

**If I break one, call it out in the chat.**

## Graphic
Five lines on Snow. A strikethrough animates across each on click. Final line in
Alpenglow.

## Animation
One click per strikethrough. Final line appears after the fifth.
The strikethrough is the whole visual idea — nothing else on the slide.

## Speaker notes
> "So you can relax and actually listen, here's what's not happening today."

> "One — I will not quote you a rate. Rates change hourly and anybody quoting one
> to a room of strangers has quoted a fiction."

> "Two — I will not tell you rates are about to go up. Nobody knows. Anyone who
> says they do is selling urgency."

> "Three — no countdown, no deadline, no 'this offer expires.'"

> "Four — I will not ask for your contact information to unlock anything. Every
> download today is free with no form."

> "Five — I will not pretend buying is always right."

*(Beat.)*

> "If I break one of those, call it out in the chat. Seriously."

## Transition
> "Alright. Before we start — I want to know who's in the room."
