# Slide 060

**Module:** 6 · **Time:** 29:10 · **Duration:** 40s + AMA · **Type:** Statement · **ARTIFACT #5**

## Purpose
Compress the module into a repeatable six-step procedure — and say out loud that
this skill was withheld, not difficult.

## Headline
**Artifact #5 — Compare two lenders in 60 seconds.**

## On-slide content
1. Both Loan Estimates issued the **same day**
2. Same **loan amount**, same **program**, same **lock period**
3. Compare **Section A only** — that's the lender
4. Note the **points** on page 1 — a bought-down rate is not a lower rate
5. Check **Cash to Close**, page 1
6. Ignore B, E, F, G — that isn't the lender competing

> **It took four minutes to teach. It has been kept from you for no reason other
> than that nobody's incentive was to teach it.**
