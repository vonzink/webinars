## Headline
**Half a point ≈ $140/month ≈ $23,000 of house.**

## Speaker notes
> "Maya and Dev's loan. Four twenty-seven five, thirty-year fixed. Here's what
> different rates do to principal and interest."

*(Read the range out loud — five point five to seven point five.)*

> "Now memorize this **shape**, because it's portable. **On a loan around this size,
> half a percent of rate is roughly a hundred forty dollars a month — and a hundred
> forty a month is roughly twenty-three thousand dollars of purchase price.**"

*(Beat.)*

> "That's the useful conversion. Because when rates move half a point, the question
> isn't 'should I panic.' The question is: **do I still want this house at
> twenty-three thousand dollars more — or do I want a house that costs twenty-three
> thousand less?**"

> "That's a decision you can actually make. 'Rates went up' is not a decision. It's
> just weather."

## Transition
> "Alright. Now the number next to your rate on every disclosure, that nobody can
> explain."
