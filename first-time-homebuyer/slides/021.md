# Slide 021

**Module:** 3 · **Time:** 8:50 · **Duration:** 35s · **Type:** Build

## Purpose
Define PITI and — more importantly — kill the myth that a fixed rate means a fixed
payment.

## Headline
**Your payment has four parts. Most people know one.**

## On-slide content
**P** — Principal · *the part that actually buys the house*
**I** — Interest · *rent on the money*
**T** — Taxes · *property taxes, collected monthly*
**I** — Insurance · *homeowners insurance, same arrangement*

> **That arrangement is called escrow.** It's a subscription your lender runs for
> you. Not a fee. **And it changes.**

## Graphic
The payment as a stacked horizontal bar, four segments building in. Semantic colors
per `branding/colors.md`: Principal = Summit Navy, Interest = Clay, Taxes = Ridge
Blue, Insurance = Stone. **These colors are fixed for the rest of the deck.**

## Animation
One segment per click, left to right. Escrow callout on the final click.

## Speaker notes
> "P-I-T-I. Four parts."

> "**Principal** — the part that actually buys the house. **Interest** — rent on the
> money. **Taxes** — your property taxes. **Insurance** — homeowners insurance."

> "That last arrangement, where your lender collects your taxes and insurance
> monthly and pays those bills for you, is called **escrow**. And it's the most
> misunderstood thing in this business."

> "Escrow is a subscription your lender runs on your behalf. They take a twelfth of
> your taxes and insurance every month, hold it, and pay the bills when they're
> due. **It is not a fee. It's not their money.**"

*(Beat.)*

> "But here's the part that surprises people: **your payment will change.** When the
> tax bill changes — usually up, usually once a year — your escrow adjusts. 'Fixed
> rate' does not mean 'fixed payment.' Now it won't be a surprise."

## Transition
> "Okay. Let's stop being abstract. For the rest of this webinar, we're buying one
> house."
