# Slide 031

**Module:** 4 · **Time:** 13:05 · **Duration:** 15s · **Type:** Poll ⭐ DIAGNOSTIC

## Purpose
Get the room to commit to a wrong answer **publicly** before the correction. A
prediction error encodes far more strongly than being told the fact cold.

## Headline
**How much do you think you need down on a $450,000 house?**

## On-slide content
**POLL 3**
- 3% — $13,500
- 5% — $22,500
- 10% — $45,000
- 20% — $90,000
- I have no idea

## Graphic
Poll overlay.

## Animation
Platform-native. **Configure the platform to HOLD results — do not auto-reveal.**

## Speaker notes
> "Quick one. Don't overthink it — what's in your head right now?"

*(20 seconds.)*

🔴 **CRITICAL: hold the results on screen without commenting.** Let them sit.
Do not react, do not hint. Then advance to 032.

The entire teaching value of the next slide depends on the audience having
committed to an answer they can watch be corrected. If you editorialize here, the
reveal is spent.

## Transition
*(Silent. Advance.)*
