# Slide 002

**Module:** 1 · **Time:** 0:20 · **Duration:** 25s · **Type:** Statement

## Purpose
Establish credibility fast and **state the bias up front**, so the audience can
stop scanning for the pitch and start listening.

## Headline
**I'm Zach. Here's my bias, up front.**

## On-slide content
- Mortgage Loan Originator · Mountain State Financial Group
- **Independent broker** — I shop lenders instead of working for one
- I build mortgage software. You'll see three of my tools today.
- I think an educated borrower is a better client. That's the whole business model.

## Graphic
Single portrait, left third — natural, not a headshot-with-crossed-arms. Right
two-thirds: four plain lines of text on Snow. No logo wall, no credential badges.

## Animation
Lines fade in on a single click, together. Not one at a time — this is 25 seconds,
not a build.

## Speaker notes
> "Zach Zink. I'm a mortgage loan originator with Mountain State Financial Group,
> here in Colorado. I'm an independent broker, which means I shop lenders instead
> of working for one. I also build mortgage software — calculators, tools — and
> you're going to see three of them today, because they explain this better than I
> can with words."

> "Here's my actual bias, stated up front: I think an educated borrower is a better
> client. Not a more profitable one on any single deal — a better one. That's the
> whole business model. That's why this is free and why nothing in the next forty
> minutes is going to feel like a pitch."

*(Hard stop at 25 seconds. Do not tell your origin story.)*
