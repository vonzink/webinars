## Purpose
Deliver Colorado-specific value that isn't available on national sites — as a
**procedure**, not a list, so it can't go stale.

## Headline
**Four layers of down payment help. Almost nobody checks all four.**

## On-slide content
**1. State — CHFA**
First mortgage + down payment assistance (grant or second mortgage). Income and
purchase price limits by county. Homebuyer education required. Participating
lenders only.

**2. Metro / city** — several Front Range municipalities run their own
**3. County** — varies widely
**4. Employer / profession** — teachers, first responders, healthcare, large
employers, unions, credit unions

> **Terms, limits, and available funding change — some run out mid-year.**
> **Ask your lender to run all four layers for your county and income, on the day
> you're shopping.**

## Graphic
Colorado map outline. Four translucent overlays stack onto it as each layer is

icon layer). The stacking metaphor is the argument.

## Animation

## Speaker notes
> "This is the part you cannot find on a national website, and it's why I made a
> Colorado-specific slide."

> "**Layer one — CHFA.** The Colorado Housing and Finance Authority. They pair a
> first mortgage with down payment assistance, either as a grant or as a second
> mortgage, layered on top of conventional, FHA, VA, or USDA. Income and purchase
> price limits by county. Homebuyer education required. And you need a
> participating lender — so ask."

> "**Layer two — metro and city.** Several Front Range municipalities run their own
> programs, sometimes with better terms than the state one."

> "**Layer three — county.** Varies enormously. Worth ten minutes."

> "**Layer four — and this is the one nobody checks — employer and profession.**
> Teachers, first responders, healthcare workers. Some large employers. Unions,
> credit unions. **Nobody ever asks their HR department whether their employer has
> a housing benefit. Some of you have one. It costs you one email to find out.**"

*(Beat. Tone shift — this next part is an obligation.)*

> "Here's my obligation to you. **Program terms, income limits, and available
> funding change, and some of these run out of money mid-year.** Anything specific
> I told you today could be stale by the time you act on it."

> "So the artifact isn't a list of programs. It's the instruction: **ask your lender
> to run the DPA layers for your county and your income, on the day you're actually
> shopping.** A broker can check across all of them. Ask by name."

## Compliance
🔴 **HIGHEST-RISK SLIDE IN THE DECK.** Never state a specific income limit, price
limit, or assistance amount without verification for the delivery date. Never imply
funding is available. Confirm participating-lender status before claiming access.

always safe.
