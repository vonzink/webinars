# Slide 057

**Module:** 6 · **Time:** 27:10 · **Duration:** 45s · **Type:** Exercise

## Purpose
Convert the just-taught skill into a motor habit through immediate retrieval. The
audience *finds* it themselves rather than being shown it.

## Headline
**45 seconds. Find the money.**

## On-slide content
Two Loan Estimates. Same loan amount. Same rate. Same day.
**Put the section letter in the chat.**

**[ 00:45 ]**

## Graphic
Split screen, two full sample page-2s at readable size. A live 45-second countdown
in the corner. No highlighting — **they have to find it.**

## Animation
Timer runs. On expiry, Section A highlights in Alpenglow on both.

## Speaker notes
> "Two Loan Estimates. Same loan amount. Same rate. Same day. Forty-five seconds —
> find the money. Put the section letter in the chat."

*(**Let the timer run. Say nothing for at least 30 seconds.** Watch chat. Read a
couple of answers aloud as they come in — it makes people brave.)*

*(At expiry, highlight.)*

> "**Section A.** Every time."

> "That's the muscle I want you to build. When somebody hands you a Loan Estimate,
> your eyes go to **page two, Section A**, before they go anywhere else. Before the
> rate. Before the payment. Before anything."
