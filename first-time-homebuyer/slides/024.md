# Slide 024

**Module:** 3 · **Time:** 10:35 · **Duration:** 30s · **Type:** Statement

## Purpose
Define DTI in under 10 words and give it a physical analogy that makes the
indifference of the ratio intuitive.

## Headline
**DTI: the ratio that runs your life.**

## On-slide content
> **Everything you're contractually obligated to pay each month**
> **÷ your gross monthly income (before taxes)**

*It's a weight limit on a suitcase. It doesn't care what's in the bag.*

## Graphic
An overpacked suitcase on an airport scale, the needle in the red. Formula
overlaid, large.

## Animation
Formula, then the analogy line on click.

## Speaker notes
> "**Debt-to-income.** It is the single number that decides how much house you get,
> and almost nobody outside this business can define it."

> "Take everything you're contractually obligated to pay every month. Divide by
> your gross monthly income — that's before taxes, not what lands in your account.
> That percentage is your DTI."

*(Beat.)*

> "Think of it like a **weight limit on a suitcase.** The airline doesn't care
> what's in the bag. It cares what it weighs. Underwriting doesn't care whether
> that five hundred dollars is a car you love or a car you regret. It's five
> hundred dollars."

## Transition
> "There are two versions of it, and one of them is the one that matters."
