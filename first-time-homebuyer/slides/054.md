# Slide 054

**Module:** 6 · **Time:** 25:15 · **Duration:** 30s · **Type:** Statement

## Purpose
Establish that the form is standardized (so comparison is easy) and that asking for
one costs nothing (removing the reason people don't shop).

## Headline
**Every lender in America uses the same form.**

## On-slide content
The **Loan Estimate** — 3 pages, federally standardized since 2015.

**Two things you're owed:**
1. It must arrive within **3 business days** of a completed application
2. Certain costs are subject to **tolerance limits** — if they go up improperly, the
   lender generally has to cure the difference

> **Asking for a Loan Estimate does not obligate you to anything.**
> **Get three. Genuinely, get three.**

## Speaker notes
> "The Loan Estimate. Three pages, federally standardized since 2015. **Every lender
> in America has to give you the same form, in the same order, with the same
> boxes.**"

> "That's the gift. Before this existed, every lender had their own layout, which
> made comparison basically impossible. Now they're identical — which means
> comparison is trivial. *If you know where to look.*"

> "Two things you're owed. **One** — you must receive it within three business days
> of a completed application. **Two** — certain costs on it are subject to tolerance
> limits. They can't just go up. Some can't increase at all without a valid changed
> circumstance; others are limited in aggregate. If they do, the lender generally
> has to cure the difference."

*(Beat.)*

> "And the sentence that matters most: **asking for a Loan Estimate does not
> obligate you to anything.** It's not a commitment. It's not a hard sell. It's a
> disclosure you're entitled to."

> "**Get three.** Genuinely. Get three."
