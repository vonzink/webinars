# Slide 010

**Module:** 2 · **Time:** 3:20 · **Duration:** 40s · **Type:** Build · **HERO**

## Purpose
Install the container that every other module gets filed into. This is the single
most-reused graphic in the deck.

## Headline
**The nine stages. House hunting is third.**

## On-slide content
1. **Financial readiness** — credit pulled, income and assets reviewed
2. **Pre-approval** — a real one
3. **House hunting** — with a budget and a letter in hand
4. **Offer & contract** — earnest money, deadlines, contingencies
5. **Loan application** — Loan Estimate within 3 business days
6. **Processing** — documents, verifications, appraisal ordered
7. **Underwriting** — the decision, then conditions
8. **Clear to close** — Closing Disclosure 3 business days before signing
9. **Closing** — sign, fund, record, keys

## Graphic
**HERO ASSET.** Horizontal 9-stage track, left to right. Stage 3 marked in
Alpenglow. Reused as the footer progress indicator throughout the deck and printed
as download #1. Build it properly once. Spec in `diagrams/`.

## Animation
Stages appear in sequence, fast (~150ms each). Then stage 3 pulses in Alpenglow.

## Speaker notes
> "Nine stages. This is the whole thing."

*(Walk it briskly — name each, don't explain. Depth comes later.)*

> "Financial readiness. Pre-approval. House hunting. Offer. Application. Processing.
> Underwriting. Clear to close. Closing."

*(Beat, then point at stage 3.)*

> "Notice where 'look at houses' is. **Third.** Not first."

> "The whole industry lets you believe it's first, because looking at houses is fun
> and getting pre-approved feels like a test. That's **mistake number one** — hold
> onto that, we're going to come back to it at the end."

## Transition
> "So let's talk about stage one, because it's the one people dread most and it's
> the least scary thing on this list."
