# Slide 011

**Module:** 2 · **Time:** 4:00 · **Duration:** 30s · **Type:** Statement

## Purpose
Remove the fear of rejection — the most common **unspoken** objection in the room,
and the one that costs the audience the most.

## Headline
**"No" is almost never the answer.**

## On-slide content
The first call is **not** an application.
Nothing is committed. Nothing is judged.

> **The real answer is: "Not yet — and here's exactly what changes it."**

Typical "not yet" = **3 to 9 months** of specific, boring, do-able steps.

## Graphic
Snow background. The headline enormous. The "not yet" line in Alpenglow below it,
in a quiet rule-bounded box. Nothing else.

## Animation
Headline, then the "not yet" line on click.

## Speaker notes
> "The first call with a lender is not an application. Nothing is committed. Nobody
> is judging you. It's twenty minutes of: here's what I make, here's what I owe,
> here's what I've saved — what does that make possible?"

> "And I'll tell you the thing that actually keeps people from making that call.
> They're afraid of hearing no."

*(Beat.)*

> "So let me take that away. **'No' is almost never the answer. 'Not yet, and here's
> exactly what changes it' is the answer.** In my experience the average 'not yet'
> is somewhere between three and nine months of specific, boring, do-able steps.
> Pay this down. Wait out that. Document this."

> "The only way that becomes three *years* is if you never made the call."

## Transition
> "Now — 'pre-approval' is actually three different things, and the difference
> matters enormously if you're going to be competing for a house."

## Compliance
"Not yet" framing describes general experience — **not a promise of approval or a
specific timeline.** Phrasing "in my experience" and "average" must be kept.
