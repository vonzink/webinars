## The ordering rule — do not change it

**Value first, unconditionally. Then the ask.**

Homework → downloads → *then* the three doors. The downloads require no email, no
form, no gate. This is deliberate and it is the entire thesis of the webinar
compressed into ninety seconds: if the content was good, the trust is already
earned, and gating it would retroactively make the last 38 minutes a lead magnet.

## 38:30 — HOMEWORK (7 items)

> "Seven things. If you do the first three this week you will be further along
> than most people are three months in."

1. **Pull your credit** — a free annual report from the official federal site.
   Look for errors. Do it now, not the week you apply.
2. **Run the Payment-Shock Test** — transfer the difference between your rent and
   your projected all-in payment into savings, every month, for three months. Find
   out if it's livable, and get paid to find out.
3. **Write down your Number** — the comfortable payment from minute eight. Put it
   somewhere you'll see it when a house makes you emotional.
4. **Call one lender.** Not to apply. Twenty minutes: here's what I make, here's
   what I owe, what's possible? "No" is almost never the answer.
5. **List your debts** — balance, minimum payment, rate. That's your DTI numerator
   and you can't improve what you haven't written down.
6. **Ask about assistance** — have someone run the CHFA / county / city / employer
   layers for your county and income.
7. **If you served, or your spouse did — get your Certificate of Eligibility.**
   Do it even if you're "not sure it counts."

## 39:10 — THE THREE DOORS

> "And then, if you want anything from me — three doors, and they're sized
> differently on purpose, because Poll 1 told me this room is in about five
> different places."

### 🚪 Door 1 — Nothing. Take the kit and go.
> "Genuinely. You now know more than most buyers do at closing. That was the point.
> If I never hear from you, this still worked."

### 🚪 Door 2 — Send me a Loan Estimate. Any lender. Free. No strings.
> "This is the one I actually want you to take."

> "If you have a Loan Estimate from **any** lender — mine, theirs, the builder's,
> the bank your parents use — send it to me. I will mark it up and send it back.
> Section A circled. Points identified. Cash to close explained. What I'd ask them.
> No cost, no obligation, and **I don't care whether I'm your lender.**"

> "I'll tell you exactly why I do this, because 'free' should always make you
> suspicious. Two reasons. One, if you send me a good Loan Estimate and I tell you
> it's good, you'll remember who told you the truth when it cost them something.
> Two, I've seen enough bad ones that I'd rather do this than watch another
> first-time buyer overpay by four figures because nobody read page two.
> That's the whole pitch. That's the most aggressive thing I'm going to do today."

### 🚪 Door 3 — A 20-minute Numbers Call.
> "No credit pull. No application. No commitment. You tell me income, debts,
> savings; I tell you what's actually possible, which programs fit, and what
> 'not yet' would look like if that's the honest answer. If the answer is 'keep
> renting for eight months and here's why,' I'll say that — I've said it plenty of
> times and I'd rather say it now than after you're in escrow."

## 40:00–55:00 — LIVE Q&A

- Moderator queues questions; take the **most-upvoted first**, then oldest.
- Answer in **≤60 seconds** or say "that one's specific to your file, let's take it
  offline" — one long answer starves ten people.
- **Rate quote requests:** the scripted redirect in `speaker_notes/delivery-guide.md`.
  Never quote a rate live.
- **Out of lane** (legal, tax, contract interpretation): say so, name who to ask.
  This is a trust asset, not a weakness.
- Unanswered questions get answered by email within 48 hours. Say that out loud
  so leaving early isn't a penalty.
- Anticipated questions, pre-drafted: `knowledge/faq.md`.

## Follow-up sequence (post-event)

| When | What | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| +2 hours | Replay link, all 5 downloads, homework list, unanswered-question answers | No ask. Value only. |
| +3 days | The one-page summary: 5 numbers, 3 documents, 1 decision | Soft mention of Doors 2 and 3 |
| +10 days | "Send me a Loan Estimate" reminder — the no-strings door only | Still no pressure |
| +30 days | Next webinar invite + "reply with any question" | Open door, no sequence |

**Hard rule:** no urgency language in any follow-up. No "rates are rising." No
"limited spots." Slide 005 was a promise made on camera; breaking it in email is
still breaking it.
