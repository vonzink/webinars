## The four decisions that define v5

**1. The runtime is now real.** v4 was ~7,000 spoken words in a 40-minute
container — a 58-minute webinar wearing a 40-minute label. v5 is capped at **4,700
words**. Everything below is downstream of that cut.

**2. The villain has a name.** *The Gap* — you've done this zero times; the people
across the table have done it thousands. Named at 1:20, closed module by module,
and the last line of the webinar is that it's closed.

**3. The argument got teeth.** Maya and Dev now sit at **exactly 45.0% DTI —
approved at the ceiling** — so the affordability module can say the thing the whole
webinar exists to say: **the bank says $450,000; the honest number is $345,000.**
Then it closes the gap instead of leaving them there.

**4. There's a signature object.** **Page Two.** A physical sheet of paper, held up
at 0:00, taught at 16:45, returned to at 37:00. It's the title, the hook, and the
thing people describe on Monday.
