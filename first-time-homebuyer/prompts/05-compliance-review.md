# Prompt 05 — Compliance Review

**Run before EVERY delivery, and after any edit that touches a number.**

> This prompt supports internal review. It is **not legal advice and does not
> replace review by qualified compliance counsel.** Final sign-off is a human's.

---

```
You are conducting a compliance review of mortgage education content produced by a
licensed loan originator at a mortgage brokerage. The content is EDUCATIONAL and is
intended to be NON-ADVERTISING. Your job is to find anything that could
recharacterize it as an advertisement, an offer, or a commitment to lend — or that
is simply inaccurate.

Report every finding with slide number, the exact language, why it's a problem, and
suggested replacement language.

## 1. TRIGGER TERMS AND ADVERTISING (TILA / Reg Z)
Flag any statement of specific credit terms that could trigger additional required
disclosures if the content were deemed an advertisement:
- Specific rates, APRs, payment amounts, down payment amounts, terms, or number of
  payments presented as AVAILABLE rather than as illustration
- Any figure not clearly labeled hypothetical ON THE SLIDE ITSELF (not just spoken,
  not just in the notes)
- Any implication that a stated rate or term is obtainable by the viewer

## 2. OFFERS AND COMMITMENTS
- Any language implying approval, guaranteed eligibility, or a commitment to lend
- Any statement that a viewer "will" or "can" get a specific outcome
- Any implied promise about a FUTURE refinance, future rates, or future pricing
  (this includes "marry the house, date the rate" and every variant)

## 3. UDAAP — unfair, deceptive, abusive
- Urgency, scarcity, deadlines, countdowns
- Any prediction of rate or price direction
- Fear-based framing
- Anything that overstates a benefit or understates a cost
- Comparisons that are technically true but leave a misleading impression

## 4. ACCURACY OF PROGRAM CLAIMS
For every statement about FHA, VA, USDA, conventional, or state/local assistance:
- Is it current? (Check MIP duration, funding fee, guarantee fee, income limits,
  loan limits, assistance program terms and FUNDING AVAILABILITY)
- Is it stated as a general guideline rather than as an individual eligibility
  determination?
- Are lender overlays acknowledged where the guideline alone would mislead?
- Is anything stated as a hard rule that is actually AUS-driven or discretionary?
  (Especially DTI caps and appreciation-based MI removal.)

## 5. FAIR LENDING
- Any language that could discourage a protected class from applying
- Any implication that certain borrowers shouldn't apply
- Equal Housing Lender mark present where required
- Imagery: does the visual representation of borrowers reflect a broad audience?

## 6. LICENSING AND IDENTIFICATION
- Individual NMLS ID present and correct
- Company name and company NMLS ID present and correct
- Placement on the required slides (open, close, and any slide with credit terms)
- State licensing scope — is anything said that implies service outside licensed
  states?
- Any unfilled placeholders (e.g. "[NMLS #______]") — these are BLOCKERS

## 7. PRIVACY AND THIRD PARTIES
- Any client story identifiable? (Should be composites with changed details.)
- Any sample document containing real consumer data? (Must be fabricated and
  watermarked SAMPLE.)
- Any competitor named or characterized?
- Any statement that could be read as disparaging a specific company?

## 8. RECORDING AND DISTRIBUTION
- Do the disclosures given at registration cover the recording and replay?
- Are the downloadable materials compliant standalone, separated from the spoken
  context that qualified them?

## 9. SCOPE OF ADVICE
- Any legal advice? (Contract interpretation, title, ownership structure)
- Any tax advice? (Deductions, 401k withdrawals, capital gains)
- Any investment advice? (Whether real estate is a good investment)
Each must be redirected to the appropriate professional.

## OUTPUT
BLOCKER / MAJOR / MINOR per finding, with slide number, exact quoted language, the
problem, and replacement language. List BLOCKERs first. State plainly whether the
content is cleared to deliver.
```

## Standing compliance decisions in this deck

Do not "clean these up" — they are load-bearing:

| Element | Why it's there |
|---|---|
| **Slide 005** — the five "I will not" promises | An on-camera commitment against trigger terms and urgency. Makes a violation visible to the audience. |
| Every rate labeled hypothetical **on-slide** | This is what keeps illustrative figures from reading as advertised terms. |
| "commonly," "can stretch to," "generally," "varies by program" | Distinguishes guideline from guarantee. Removing these hedges creates claims. |
| MI removal framed with "servicer and investor rules vary" | Appreciation-based removal is discretionary, not an entitlement. |
| Slide 067's stated conflict of interest | Fair characterization of builder arrangements + honest disclosure. |
| No competitor named anywhere | Defamation and disparagement exposure. |
| Stories are composites | Privacy. |
| CHFA slide teaches the *procedure*, not a program list | Program terms and funding change; a stale list sends people to a closed program. |

## Known BLOCKERs before first delivery

- [ ] `[NMLS #______]` placeholders — individual and company — appear in README,
      PROJECT, slide 001, slide 070. **All must be filled.**
- [ ] All 🔴 `VERIFY` flags in `research/` cleared
- [ ] CHFA terms and funding availability confirmed for the delivery date
- [ ] Sample Loan Estimate graphics use fabricated data and are watermarked
