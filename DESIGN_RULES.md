# DESIGN_RULES.md
## Mountain State Financial Group Presentation Standards

Version 1.0

These rules are mandatory.

If any design decision conflicts with these rules, these rules always take precedence.

---

# PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

Create the most beautiful first-time homebuyer presentation ever produced by a mortgage company.

The audience should immediately think:

"This feels like Apple designed a mortgage presentation."

Not

"This looks like PowerPoint."

---

# DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Every slide should communicate:

Confidence

Trust

Simplicity

Professionalism

Clarity

Luxury

Modern Technology

Education

Every slide should have breathing room.

Never design to fill space.

Design to guide attention.

---

# GOLDEN RULE

One slide.

One idea.

Never teach multiple concepts on the same slide.

---

# VISUAL HIERARCHY

A viewer should understand the slide in this order:

1 Title

2 Primary visual

3 Supporting headline

4 Supporting content

5 Footer

Nothing should compete with the title.

---

# GRID SYSTEM

Everything aligns.

Nothing floats randomly.

Use consistent margins.

Use consistent spacing.

Never eyeball alignment.

---

# WHITESPACE

Whitespace is not empty.

Whitespace is a design element.

Never fill unused space with decorations.

---

# TYPOGRAPHY

Titles should be large.

Very large.

Use typography as a design element.

Body text should rarely exceed 25 words.

Prefer bullets over paragraphs.

Never use tiny fonts.

Minimum body size:

22pt

Preferred:

26–32pt

Titles:

48–72pt

Section titles:

72–96pt

---

# COLORS

Use only official brand colors.

Primary

#104547

Secondary

#4B7B4D

Accent

#8CC63E

Neutral

#404041

White

Avoid additional colors unless required for charts.

---

# ICONS

Every icon must belong to the same family.

Never mix styles.

Never mix outline and filled icons.

Prefer thin modern icons.

---

# PHOTOGRAPHY

Photography should feel authentic.

Avoid obvious stock photography.

Avoid fake smiling business people.

Preferred imagery:

Colorado

Real neighborhoods

Families

Homeownership

Architecture

Lifestyle

Natural light

Use photographs with room for text.

---

# IMAGES

Images should support the story.

Never decorate.

Crop intentionally.

Use subtle shadows.

Never use heavy borders.

Never stretch images.

---

# ILLUSTRATIONS

Illustrations should be:

Flat

Modern

Simple

Editorial

Avoid cartoon styles.

Avoid childish graphics.

---

# INFOGRAPHICS

Replace paragraphs whenever possible.

Show.

Don't tell.

Build diagrams instead of lists.

---

# CHARTS

Charts should be minimal.

No unnecessary gridlines.

No 3D charts.

No exploding pie charts.

No rainbow colors.

Only brand colors.

---

# TABLES

Avoid large tables.

Break comparisons into cards whenever possible.

---

# CALCULATORS

Calculators are premium features.

Slides should introduce the calculator.

Then transition naturally into the live demo.

The calculator should feel integrated.

---

# ANIMATIONS

Animations should guide attention.

Not entertain.

Preferred:

Fade

Morph

Appear

Wipe

Zoom

Avoid:

Bounce

Spin

Fly

Flash

Random motion

---

# TRANSITIONS

Slides should feel connected.

Avoid abrupt design changes.

Maintain visual rhythm.

---

# SECTION DIVIDERS

Every major section deserves its own slide.

Use large typography.

Minimal text.

Strong imagery.

---

# TEXT

Never write walls of text.

Maximum bullet count:

Five

Maximum bullet length:

One sentence.

---

# EMPHASIS

Use color sparingly.

If everything is highlighted, nothing is highlighted.

---

# BRANDING

Branding should feel premium.

Never oversized.

Never repetitive.

The logo appears once per slide at most.

Many slides require no logo.

---

# FOOTERS

Use consistent footer placement.

Include compliance information only when appropriate.

Do not clutter educational slides.

---

# ACCESSIBILITY

Maintain high contrast.

Never place text over busy backgrounds.

Always prioritize readability.

---

# SPEAKER NOTES

Every slide should include:

Objective

Talking points

Transition

Audience question

Time estimate

---

# AUDIENCE INTERACTION

Approximately every five slides:

Ask a question.

Tell a story.

Use a calculator.

Poll the audience.

Show an example.

Keep people involved.

---

# EMOTIONAL FLOW

The presentation should follow this journey:

Welcome

Curiosity

Confidence

Understanding

Empowerment

Excitement

Action

---

# MORTGAGE PHILOSOPHY

We educate.

We do not pressure.

We simplify.

We build confidence.

We explain the "why."

---

# QUALITY TEST

Before approving any slide ask:

Would Apple present this?

Would TED use this?

Would this look impressive on a 120-inch projector?

Would someone screenshot this slide?

Would this make attendees trust Mountain State Financial Group more?

If any answer is "No"

Redesign the slide.

---

# FINAL RULE

Good enough is unacceptable.

Every slide should feel handcrafted.

Every element should have purpose.

Every presentation should become the new standard for mortgage education.
