# PROJECT_GOALS.md
## Mountain State Financial Group
### First-Time Homebuyer Webinar

---

# Project Name

The Homebuyer's Playbook

A Premium Educational Seminar for First-Time Homebuyers

---

# Mission

Build the most engaging, informative, and visually stunning first-time homebuyer presentation ever created by a mortgage company.

This presentation should become Mountain State Financial Group's flagship educational resource and establish the company as the trusted mortgage educator in every market we serve.

This is not a sales presentation.

This is a premium educational experience.

---

# Primary Objective

Help first-time homebuyers confidently answer one question:

"Can I actually buy a home?"

By the end of the presentation attendees should understand:

• How mortgages work

• What they can realistically afford

• The buying process

• Available loan programs

• Down payment assistance

• Credit requirements

• Closing costs

• Interest rates

• How to avoid expensive mistakes

They should leave feeling educated, empowered, and excited—not overwhelmed.

---

# Business Goals

The webinar should naturally generate qualified mortgage leads by establishing trust.

The audience should voluntarily want to work with Mountain State Financial Group because they believe we are the most knowledgeable, transparent, and helpful mortgage professionals they have encountered.

The presentation should create:

• Consultation requests

• Mortgage applications

• Realtor referrals

• Repeat attendees

# Presentation Principles

Every slide must answer one question.

Every slide should teach one concept.

Every slide should simplify something complicated.

Never overwhelm.

Never lecture.

Never read from the slide.

The slides support the presenter.

The presenter tells the story.

---

# Learning Philosophy

People remember stories.

People remember visuals.

People remember demonstrations.

People forget bullet points.

Whenever possible:

Show.

Illustrate.

Animate.

Demonstrate.

Use calculators.

Use examples.

Tell stories.

Avoid long paragraphs.

---

# Live Demonstrations

Throughout the presentation integrate live demonstrations using:

Mortgage Payment Calculator

Rent vs Buy Calculator

Affordability Calculator

Budget Calculator

Debt-to-Income Calculator

APR Calculator

Interest Rate Buydown Calculator

Cash-to-Close Calculator

Amortization Calculator

Each calculator should be introduced with context before switching to the live demonstration.

When returning from the calculator, summarize the key takeaway before moving on.

Every slide should be visually memorable.
